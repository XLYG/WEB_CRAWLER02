# LLM in Legal Theory

### General Information

The European Academy of Legal Theory offers a one-year (60 ECTS) advanced Master's Course in Legal Theory (LL.M.; language of instruction: English). Participants will study at one or more Academy partner universities (mobility phases).

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **AWARD:** | LL.M. in Legal Theory |
| **DURATION:** | 2 semesters |
| **WORK LOAD:** | 60 ECTS |
| **LANGUAGE:** | English |
| **FEES:** | EUR 7.200,- |
| **START:** | Every year in October |
| **APPLICATION:** | Please use the application form available in the download box |
| **DEADLINE:** | September 15th |

The Academy's LLM-Programme in Legal Theory is hosted at the [Goethe-University](http://www.jura.uni-frankfurt.de/35393027/fb01?legacy_request=1) in Frankfurt/Main. It starts every year in October and welcomes applicants from all over the world holding a basic law degree or a basic degree in a subject related to legal theory and having completed one year of professional and/or research experience.
