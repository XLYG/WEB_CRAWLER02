## § 1Scope 

These Admissions Regulations (Zugangs- und Zulassungsordnung) regulate the admissions process for the Master's programme in Knowledge and Communication at the University of Münster.


## § 2Dates, Deadlines and Documents 

(1)1Admission to the Master's programme in Knowledge and Communication is only possible at the beginning of the winter semester of each academic year. 2Admissions applications (Antrag auf Zulassung) are to be submitted to the Student Admissions Office (Studierendensekretariat) of the University of Münster. 3The deadlines for submitting applications are governed by the Regulations for the Allocation of Study Places in North Rhine-Westphalia [Verordnung über die Vergabe von Studienplätzen in NordrheinWestfalen - VergabeVO NRw] and the Regulations Governing the Settlement of Admissions Procedure Issues at the University of Münster [Ordnung zur Regelung zulassungsrechtlicher Fragen in der Universität Münster]. “Applications must be submitted via the online application portal of the University of Münster. 5Applicants must submit/upload the following documents in German or English with their application:

1. Proof of a general university entrance qualification or of an appropriate subject-specific university entrance qualification 



2.1Proof of completion of an undergraduate professional qualification in accordance with § 4 (1). 2If, at the time of application, the degree certificate in accordance with § 4 (1)is not yet available, a provisional certificate must be submitted on the basis of a degree programme in accordance with § 4 (1) in which the applicant has completed at least the first five semesters and has earned at least 120 credits (Leistungspunkte). 3The provisional certificate must contain an average grade that meets the requirements for the final grade in accordance with § 4 (1) and that has been calculated on the basis of at least 120 credits. 4An official certificate as referred to in no. 3 sentence 1 is also considered to be a provisional certificate if it meets the content requirements of sentences 2 and 3. 5If the provisional certificate as referred to in sentence 2 or the official certificate as referred to in sentence 4 does not indicate an average grade, this must be documented separately. 6The degree certificate in accordance with § 4 (1)must be presented at enrolment should the applicant be granted admission to the master's programme. 7Applicants who did not obtain their undergraduate professional qualification from a German-speaking or English-speaking institution must also provide an official German or English version or an officially certified translation of the above documents.



3.1Official proof of all required coursework and degree-relevant examinations (Studienund Prüfungsleistungen) (e.g. Transcript of Records) completed within the programme as defined in § 4 (1) (including such coursework and examinations completed during the orientation phase and similar phases of study, regardless of whether they contribute to the final grade), stating the number of credits. ²Applicants who did not obtain their undergraduate professional qualification in accordance with § 4 (1) from a German-speaking or English-speaking institution must also provide an official German or English version or an officially certified translation of the above proof. ³The degree certificate in accordance with no. 2 sentence 1 or the provisional certificate in accordance with no. 2 sentence 2 may also serve as proof in accordance with sentence 
1, provided that it confirms completion of the coursework or examinations referred to in sentence 1.



4. If the credit system used in the programme leading to the undergraduate professional qualification in accordance with § 4 (1) does not comply with the European Credit Transfer System (ECTS), the applicant shall, using the form provided for this purpose,declare the ECTS point value of the grades obtained in the required coursework and degree-relevant examinations in accordance with no. 2 sentences 1 to 4 and no. 3sentence 1.



5. Proof of a sufficient command of English in accordance with § 4 (3)

. CV (tabular format)

7. 1Overview of the knowledge acquired in communication science and the methods of empirical social research in accordance with § 4 (1) sentences 2 to 4 in the form provided for this purpose. 2In order to ensure verifiability of the relevance of a field of study, the applicant must also submit a module handbook describing the relevant courses or a comparable official description of the relevant courses. 3For module handbooks and course descriptions that are not available in German or English, the applicant must submit an officially certified German or English translation or a translation certified by the institution where the professional qualification in accordance with § 4 (1) is being or was obtained.



8. If applicable, documents proving a specific case of hardship (Härtefall) as defined by § 6 (4) (e.g. certificate of disability (Behindertenausweis))



(2)The admissions application must be rejected if it is not submitted on time. The application may be rejected if the documents referred to in § 2 (1) are incomplete.

## § 3Selection Committee 

(1)The faculty board (Fachbereichsrat) of the Faculty of Educational and Social Sciences (FB 6) must set up a selection committee (Auswahlkommission) consisting of full-time members of the faculty to carry out the admissions process for the Master's programme in Knowledge and Communication.



(2)1The selection committee is made up of at least two full members from the group of professors and at least two full members from the group of academic staff and/or the group of non-academic staff, provided that the latter hold a doctorate. The chair and vice-chair of the selection committee must be professors. ³The number of academic or non-academic staff among the full members may not exceed the number of professors. 4A deputy should be elected for each full member from the group of academic or non-academic staff.5Deputies may belong to the group of academic and/or non-academic staff. 6The term of office for selection committee members is one year. 7Re-election is possible.

(3)1The selection committee is quorate if at least the chair or vice-chair and a member from the group of academic or non-academic staff are present. In the event of a tie, the chair or, in their absence, the vice-chair has the casting vote.


(4)1Meetings of the selection committee are not open to the public. ²Selection committee members are bound by official secrecy. 3If they are not public-sector employees, they shall be bound to secrecy by the chair. 4At the invitation of the chair, guests may attend meetings of the selection committee, who are also required to maintain secrecy. 'Guests have the right to speak, but not to propose motions or to vote.



(5)Minutes are taken to document the selection committee's application assessments and deliberations.



## Part 1: Requirements for Admission to the Master's Programme 

## § 4Admission Requirements 

(1)1Apart from the general terms for enrolment at the University of Münster, the requirement for admission to the Master's programme in Knowledge and Communication is the successful completion of an undergraduate degree programme in a relevant field of study with a minimum standard duration of six semesters; this degree programme must have resulted in a bachelor's degree or other professional qualification (Diplom, state examination, etc.). ²A degree programme at either a German university or a university abroad is considered to be in a relevant field of study as indicated in sentence 1, provided that the student acquired at least 70 credits by completing required coursework and degree-relevant examinations in the subjects of communication science or media studies.3The coursework or examinations must correspond to the content or modules of the Bachelor's programme in Communication Science at the University of Münster, or they must be an appropriate supplement to the social science orientation of the subject (topics such as media systems, media policy, media economics, media psychology, media management). “This minimum of 70 credits in a relevant field of study must meet the following reguirements:

1. A maximum of ten credits may be earned in practical media and communication courses.



2. At least ten credits must be earned in the area of communication science approaches and theories.



3. 1At least 20 credits must be earned in empirical research methods relevant to the field of study (methods of data collection and analysis/statistics). ²For these credits from the area of empirical research methods relevant to the field of study, the following applies:

i. A maximum of ten credits may be proven by empirical project seminars or research seminars or by an empirical bachelor's thesis.



ii. At least five credits must be proven in the area of statistics.

iii. Courses teaching general academic work are not recognised.
sThe credits must be clearly substantiated in accordance with § 2 (1) sentence 5 no. 3 (e.g.degree certificate or Transcript of Records) and, in accordance with § 2 (1) sentence 5 no.7, listed in a comprehensible manner in the overview of the acguired knowledge and must result from the corresponding module handbook or the corresponding course descriptions.6The selection committee decides on the relevance and accuracy of fit on the basis of the documents submitted by the applicants.



(2)1The Student Admissions Office shall determine if degrees conferred from universities outside the European Union are equivalent. 2If there is any doubt as to the equivalence of a foreign degree, an appraisal of equivalence is to be obtained from the Office of the Standing Conference of the Ministers of Education and Cultural Affairs of the Länder in the Federal Republic of Germany (KMK).



(3)1To gain admission to the Master's programme in Knowledge and Communication,applicants must demonstrate a good to very good command of English. ²English language skills equivalent to level C1 of the "Common European Framework of Reference for Languages" (CEFR) are considered sufficient. 3Evidence of sufficient knowledge of English is provided by the submission of a recognised certificate. “Recognised certificates within the meaning of sentence 4 are TOEFL, TOEIC, IELTS and CAE. 5Certificates are generally accepted as long as they were issued in a test completed no longer than three years prior to the submission deadline for applications. 6A bachelor's degree in an English-taught programme is equivalent to these language requirements. 'The selection committee decides whether the applicant has adequate language proficiency on the basis of the documents submitted.



(4)Applicants may not gain admission to the Master's programme in Knowledge and Communication if they have irrevocably failed a degree-relevant examination (Prüfungsleistung) in the Master's programme in Knowledge and Communication or a university examination or state examination in a subject considered relevant in accordance with § 4 (1).



## § 5Fulfilment of Admission Requirements 

(1)The selection committee examines the documents and certificates submitted with the admissions application in order to determine whether the applicant meets the admission requirements as defined in § 4.



(2)If a degree certificate is not yet available at the time of application, it is sufficient if the provisional certificate or the Transcript of Records (see § 2 (1) sentence 5 no. 2) contains a grade that meets the requirements for the final grade as defined in § 4 (1).

(3)If an applicant is considered not to meet the admission requirements, the reasons for the rejection must be documented.


## Part 2: Admission to the Master's Programme 

## § 6Selection Process 

(1)If the number of applicants to the Master's programme in Knowledge and Communication who meet the admission requirements as specified in § 4 does not exceed the restricted number of places available for this degree programme, the applicants who qualify for admission are to be admitted without further examination.



(2)If the number of applicants to the Master's programme in Knowledge and Communication who meet the admission requirements as specified in § 4 exceeds the restricted number of places available for this degree programme, applicants are to be selected on the basis of the grade stated in the degree certificate referred to in § 2 (1) sentence 5 no. 2.

(3)Applicants are admitted to the available places beginning with the highest grade stated in the degree certificate referred to in § 2 (1) sentence 5 no. 2. In the event of identical grades,lots are drawn to determine the position of the candidate on the ranking list.

(4)1Up to 2% of the places available are to be assigned in advance by the Student Admissions Office to applicants who qualify for admission on the basis of the hardship provisions of the Regulations for the Allocation of Study Places in North Rhine-Westphalia [VergabeVO NRW]. ²The ranking is determined by the level of exceptional hardship; in cases of doubt,lots are drawn.



## Part 3: Final Provisions 

## § 7Conclusion of Admissions Process 

(1)1If an applicant meets the admission requirements and is admitted to the master's programme, they receive notification of admission (Zulassungsbescheid), issued by the Rector, immediately after completion of the process. ²In the case of § 2 (1) sentence 5 no.2, the applicant is to receive a notification of admission on the condition that the degree certificate referred to in § 4 (1) is submitted at the time of enrolment.

(2)1If the applicant is admitted to the master's programme on the basis of a ranking list, the Rector is to set a deadline by which the applicant must notify the University of their intention to accept the place offered. 2lf the applicant declines the offer, the place is to be offered to the next applicant on the ranking list, provided that places are still available. 3Ilf the applicant fails to notify the University in accordance with § 7 (2) sentence 1 by the given deadline, the offer is deemed to have been declined.



(3)1If an applicant is not admitted to the programme, the Rector is to notify them accordingly and state whether the admission requirements were met. 3The letter of rejection must specify the reasons for rejection as well as information on the legal remedies available.
(4)1The applicant can only enrol at the University of Münster if they present the notification of admission to the Student Admissions Office together with the enrolment application (Antrag auf Einschreibung) within the designated enrolment period. ²With regard to the other provisions,the current version of the Enrolment Regulations (Einschreibungsordnung) of the University of Münster applies.

## § 8Deception 

(1)1If an applicant has acted dishonestly in the admissions process or has submitted or uploaded false or fraudulent documents as per § 2 and § 4, and if this fact is only discovered after the applicant has been admitted, the offer of admission is to be revoked.2Revocation is only possible within two years of the disclosure of these facts.

(2)1The applicant must be informed immediately of any decision adverse to them, together with the reasons for the decision and information on the legal remedies available. ²The applicant must be given the opportunity to be heard before a decision is made.

Diese Ordnung regelt den Zugang und die Zulassung zum Masterstudiengang Knowledge and Communication an der Universität Münster.



## § 2Termine, Fristen und Unterlagen 

(1) 1Das Masterstudium Knowledge and Communication kann ausschließlich zum Wintersemester eines Studienjahres aufgenommen werden. ²Der Antrag auf Zulassung ist beim Studierendensekretariat der Universität Münster einzureichen. 3Die Fristen zur 
Stellung des Antrags richten sich nach der Verordnung über die Vergabe von Studienplätzen in Nordrhein-Westfalen (VergabeVo NRw) und der Ordnung zur Regelung zulassungsrechtlicher Fragen in der Universität Münster. "Die Bewerbung erfolgt über das elektronische Bewerbungsportal der Universität Münster. 5Die*der Bewerber*in muss folgende Bewerbungsunterlagen in deutscher oder englischer Sprache einreichen bzw.hochladen:

## § 4Zugangsvoraussetzungen 

(1)1Voraussetzung für den Zugang zum Masterstudiengang Knowledge and Communication ist neben den allgemeinen Voraussetzungen für die Einschreibung die Absolvierung eines fachlich einschlägigen Studiums mit einer Regelstudienzeit von mindestens 6 Semestern,das mit einem Bachelor oder einem anderen berufsqualifizierenden Abschluss (Diplom,Staatsexamen etc.) erfolgreich beendet worden ist. ²Fachlich einschlägig im Sinne von Satz 1 ist ein wissenschaftliches Studium an einer deutschen oder ausländischen Hochschule,wenn in einem der Fächer Kommunikationswissenschaft,Publizistikwissenschaft oder Medienwissenschaft Studien- und Prüfungsleistungen im Umfang von mindestens 70 Leistungspunkten erworben worden sind. 3Die Leistungen müssen äquivalent zu den im Bachelorstudium im Fach Kommunikationswissenschaft an der Universität Münster zu studierenden Inhalten bzw. Modulen sein oder die sozialwissenschaftliche Ausrichtung des Fachs sinnvoll ergänzen (Themenfelder wie z. B.Mediensystem,Medienpolitik,Medienökonomie,Medienpsychologie,Medienmanagement). “Diese fachlich einschlägigen mindestens 70 Leistungspunkte müssen folgenden Anforderungen genügen:

1. Maximal 10 Leistungspunkte dürfen in medien- und kommunikationspraktischen Kursen nachgewiesen werden.



2. Mindestens 10Leistungspunkte müssen im Bereich kommunikationswissenschaftlicher Ansätze und Theorien nachgewiesen werden.
