Apply Now

### Application Periods & Deadlines

The general deadlines for the applications are:

* Starting **winter semester** (October): **March 15th**of each year for the following winter semester. ***Important hint: the application website is open from approx. January, 25th until March 15th.***
* Starting **summer semester** (April): **October 31st** of the previous year for the following summer semester. ***Important hint: the application website is open from approx. August, 25th until October 31st.***

**Please note, that applications can only be submitted via the [WueStudy](https://wuestudy.zv.uni-wuerzburg.de/qisserver/pages/cs/sys/portal/hisinoneStartPage.faces) application system approximately one month before the above-mentioned deadlines.**

Important Hints
---------------

**You do NOT need to provide a preliminary review documentation (VPD) from [uni-assist](http://www.uni-assist.de/ "http://www.uni-assist.de/") to apply for the master programs in “Physics International" and “Quantum Engineering”.**

**Please only apply once your final grade has been determined and you can submit at least a provisional certificate, or you can already submit your final documents.**

### Can I apply without a final bachelor's certificate?

No, you can only apply once your final grade has been determined and you can submit at least a provisional certificate or your final documents.

### Do I need to provide a preliminary review documentation (VPD) from uni-assist?

You DO NOT need to provide a preliminary review documentation (VPD) from [uni-assist](http://www.uni-assist.de/ "http://www.uni-assist.de/") to apply for the master programs in “Physics International" and “Quantum Engineering”.

### Where can I track the status of my application?

You can track your application via the WueStudy application system.

### Whom should I contact for further questions?

Please communicate only via the email address [international@physik.uni-wuerzburg.de](mailto:international@physik.uni-wuerzburg.de)

### Do all documents have to be certified?

To apply via WueStudy, your documents must be translated into English, but not notarized.

### I am graduating in six months, can I apply now?

No, please do not apply until your final grade has been determined and you can submit at least a provisional certificate, or you can already submit your final certificates.

### English language skills: Is my Bachelor's degree in English sufficient for application?

You can enclose a certificate from your university confirming your English language skills with your application documents, but the examination board will check this and decide whether it is sufficient.

### My documents are not yet complete. Can I still apply?

No, please do not apply now. Please wait until you will be able to provide all required documents.

### I have not yet received a reply to my application, what can I do?

Please be patient and follow up your application via the WueStudy application system.

### Is it possible to apply at the faculty by sending documents via mail?

No, this is not possible. Please be aware that all documents sent by mail, will be trashed without further notice.
