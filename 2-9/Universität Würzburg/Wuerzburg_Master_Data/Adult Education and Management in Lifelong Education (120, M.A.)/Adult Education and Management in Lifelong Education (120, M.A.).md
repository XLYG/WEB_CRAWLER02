The Programme at a Glance
-------------------------

Our **Master’s Programme in Adult Education and Management in Lifelong Learning** is designed for students and practitioners with a **bachelor’s degree (180 ECTS)** and a foundation in educational studies amounting to at least **40 ECTS credits**. For detailed information on admission requirements and the application process, [please refer to our website](/studium/master-adult-education-and-management-in-lifelong-education-ma/application-and-admission/).

#### Language Requirements

The programme offers modules taught in English, making it accessible to individuals with limited German proficiency. Applicants are required to have at least **B2-level English skills**. **German language skills** can be acquired in the "Compulsory Electives" module.

#### Programme Format

All courses are conducted **on-campus in Würzburg**, providing a vibrant, in-person learning environment. While it is encouraged for students to relocate to Würzburg, those unable to do so should ensure their participation in the designated **on-campus intensive weeks** to fully engage with the programme. A significant portion of the courses is also delivered in a **hybrid format**, enabling remote online learning. For more details about the available courses, please visit the [Study Structure](/studium/master-adult-education-and-management-in-lifelong-education-ma/study-structure/) page.

#### On-Campus Intensive Weeks

We strongly recommend attending **intensive weeks** held (semi-)annually in Würzburg. Please find the exact dates for these weeks at the beginning of the page under "Dates".

These weeks provide opportunities to connect with peers, faculty, and practitioners, as well as to explore the campus and city:

* **First two Semester Weeks**: Recommended for newcomers to familiarize themselves with the campus (e.g., library, seminar buildings) and Würzburg.
* **Adult Education Academy**: Held annually in February each year, this event is a highlight of the programme. In 2026, it will run from **February 2–13**. The academy fosters networking with other students, practitioners, and professors, and includes visits to adult education and lifelong learning institutions in Würzburg and the surrounding region. [More details about the Adult Education Academy are available on our website.](/lifelonglearning/)

#### Double-Degree Options

In addition to the general degree programme, there is also the **option of completing a double degree** with the University of **Padua** (Double Degree in *Management of Educational Services, Adult and Continuing Education*), where the second semester can be completed in Padua, or with the University of **Ljubljana** (Double Degree in *Adult Education and Andragogy*), where students complete their third semester in Slovenia.

Further information about the double degree options can be found [here](/studium/master-adult-education-and-management-in-lifelong-education-ma/double-degree-options/).

#### Part-time Options

To give you the flexibility to optimally combine your studies with other aspects of your life (like work or care obligations), you can study this master programme completely or partly in part-time. This means generally you study for longer overall, but you attend fewer classes and take fewer exams per semester. You can find more information on studying in part-time in Appendix 4 of the General Academic and Examination Regulations (ASPO).

If you are interested in studying part-time, please consult us for a consultation.

#### Visa Information for Non-EU Applicants

Non-EU applicants are strongly advised to **schedule a visa appointment** as early as possible to ensure timely processing. Further information about the visa process is available on the [website of the German Foreign Office](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/en/visa-service/215870-215870) . Additionally, external resources (like MyGermanUniversity) can provide helpful step-by-step guides on [Student Visas](https://www.mygermanuniversity.com/articles/Student-Visa-Germany) and [Blocked Accounts](https://www.mygermanuniversity.com/blocked-account-germany). For specialized advice on visas and residence permits in Germany, you can also consult the Migrationsberatung. They offer tailored counseling services for [individuals under 27](https://beratung.jugendmigrationsdienste.de/website) and [from 27 years of age](https://www.migrationsberatung.org/en/), helping with the navigation of visa requirements and residence permits.

For further details, visit our website ([especially the Frequently Asked Questions](/studium/master-adult-education-and-management-in-lifelong-education-ma/faqs/)) or contact the programme office at [master-education@uni-wuerzburg.de](mailto:master-education@uni-wuerzburg.de).

### Study Course Options

You can complete the Master in Adult Education and Management in Lifelong Education (M.A.) in **three different ways:**

* by attending all modules in Würzburg,
* by choosing the double degree program with the University of Padua and spending one semester at the Italian university, or
* by choosing the double degree program with the University of Ljubljana and spending one semester at the Slovenian university.
