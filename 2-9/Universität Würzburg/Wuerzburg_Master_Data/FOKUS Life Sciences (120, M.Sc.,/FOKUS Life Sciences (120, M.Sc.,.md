M.Sc. FOKUS Life Sciences
=========================

The master's program **FOKUS Life Sciences** offers an interdisciplinary and fast-track preparation for pursuing a PhD at the Graduate School of Life Sciences (GSLS) at the University of Würzburg. Research topics encompass Biomedicine, Integrative Biology, Infection and Immunity, and Neuroscience, involving a wide array of [research groups](https://gsls.cloud.opencampus.net/gsls-members-pi).
