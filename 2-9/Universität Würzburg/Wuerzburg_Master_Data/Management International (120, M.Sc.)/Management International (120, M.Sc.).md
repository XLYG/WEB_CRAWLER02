Master Management International
===============================

**Management International (M.Sc.)**
------------------------------------

Enhance your skills and knowledge to build a **successful international career**. Designed for **ambitious students** with a **global outlook**, this programme is taught entirely in **English** and is dedicated to equipping you for success in a **rapidly evolving world**.

Join our programme and become part of an **engaging learning community**. Explore a curriculum that integrates **global perspectives**, **digital transformation**, and **cutting-edge expertise**, preparing you for success in a global environment.

#### [Apply now!](https://wuestudy.zv.uni-wuerzburg.de/qisserver/pages/cs/sys/portal/subMenu.faces?navigationPosition=hisinoneapp_studi_no_login&noDBAction=y&init=y)

Programme structure
-------------------

The **Master Management International** is a fully accredited two-year programme, taught entirely in English and awarding 120 ECTS credits. It is designed for students seeking an international academic experience or preparing for a global career. The program also invites top international talent aspiring to pursue an advanced degree in Germany.

Study plan Management International starting winter semester 2026/27

### First year

In the **first year**, you will build a strong foundation in advanced management principles, complemented by essential skills in economics, data science, research methods, and soft skills. This **multidisciplinary approach** prepares you to confidently address today’s complex business challenges through data-driven and informed decision-making.

### Core – Management

This area provides you with in-depth knowledge across various management disciplines. You can choose courses from a predefined pool, allowing you to tailor your learning to your individual interests while developing a comprehensive understanding of management practices.

**Credits:**25 ECTS

### Core – Economics

This area equips you with a strong foundation in economics. By selecting from a range of courses, you can deepen your understanding of economic theories and applications, building the skills needed for conducting thorough economic analyses.

**Credits:** 10 ECTS

### Core – Business Analytics

This area focuses on developing your skills in data science. You will learn to analyze data and apply quantitative methods to support well-informed and effective management decisions.

**Credits:** 10 ECTS

### Core – Career Development

This area offers the opportunity to learn important language skills with basic German and Business English courses and teaches soft skills for working in international teams. As part of your studies, you will acquire important tools for a successful start to your professional life.

**Credits:** 10 ECTS

### Core – Research

This area emphasizes essential skills in scientific work. You will learn how to present research results clearly and apply scientific methods to address complex challenges.

**Credits:** 5 ECTS

### Second year

In the **second year**, you have the opportunity to **tailor your education** to your career goals by specializing in **one of four distinct tracks**. This allows you to gain advanced expertise and focus on developing skills that align with your professional aspirations.

### Track – Finance & Accounting

This track equips you with advanced knowledge in finance, accounting, and taxation. You will develop the skills to tackle complex tasks at both national and international levels.

**Credits:**30 ECTS

**Career opportunities:**Graduates of this track are ideally qualified for careers in investment banking, controlling, auditing or financial consulting. They also have the opportunity to take on management positions in international companies.

### Track – Operations & Information Management

This track focuses on analyzing and optimizing business processes by leveraging modern technologies and data-driven strategies to enhance efficiency and drive innovation. You will gain expertise in managing complex production and information systems, enabling you to make informed, data-backed decisions.

**Credits:** 30 ECTS

**Career opportunities:**This focus opens up career opportunities in the fields of supply chain management, process optimisation, IT management and data-based management consulting. Graduates are sought-after experts who can drive the digital transformation in companies.

### Track – Marketing & Entrepreneurship

This track emphasizes the development and implementation of innovative marketing strategies alongside the fundamentals of entrepreneurship. You will learn to analyze market trends, develop creative business ideas, and navigate entrepreneurial challenges with confidence.

**Credits:** 30 ECTS

**Career opportunities:**Graduates can pursue careers in strategic marketing, product management or market research. For those with an entrepreneurial spirit, the specialisation offers an ideal basis for founding their own start-up or working in innovation management.

### Track – Behavioral Economics

This track focuses on the examination and processing of behavioral economics issues. You will learn to use data analysis methods to analyse socially relevant topics in a structured manner and draw conclusions.

**Credits:** 30 ECTS

**Career opportunities:**Graduates can pursue careers in the analysis and design of economic decision-making processes, behavioral policy evaluation, and data-driven consumer and user behavior research. The specialisation also qualifies graduates for roles in behavioral consulting and evidence-based innovation environments.

### Master's Thesis

Towards the end of the programme, you will write your Master's thesis in your previously selected track. The duration is six months.

**Credits:** 30 ECTS

How to apply
------------

### Application calendar

The **Master Management International** program can only be started in the winter semester. You can apply for the coming winter semester until 15 July. The application portal usually opens in mid to late January.

### Application requirements

**Language**

*Language requirements – English*  
 English language skills should be at the level of B2. Alternatively, language requirements can be met by different tests and certificates:

* TOEFL-Test with at least 85 Points or more
* IELTS with at least a score of 6,5 or better
* FCE Cambridge Certificate with at least 176 points
* Pearson PTE Academic Test with at least 75 points

For more information, please take a look at the examination regulations.

*Language requirements – German*  
 German language skills should be at the level of A2 by the end of the 3rd semester – not necessary for the application.

**Competencies**

Admission to the Master Management International requires a Bachelor's degree in business management or economics that covers core competencies in management, economics, and methods. The following competencies should be acquired in the Bachelor's degree programme:

* Competencies from modules amounting to at least 20 ECTS credits in the fields of methods (mathematics, statistics, econometrics, and applied data-oriented methods, e.g. data science, business analytics, data analytics)
* Competencies from modules in the amount of at least 60 ECTS credits from the field of management and economics

If you are not sure whether you meet access requirements, please feel free to contact us by e-mail: [master@wiwi.uni-wuerzburg.de](mailto:master@wiwi.uni-wuerzburg.de)

**Final grade**

Only applicants with a bachelor grade **better than 2.0** can be admitted directly.

If you do not meet the grade criterion and have a bachelor grade **better than 3.0,** you can take a [GMAT exam](https://start.mba.com/5-steps-to-gmat-success) to meet the grade criterion. You need **at least 565 points in the GMAT Focus test exam** in this case.

### Required documents

**Required documents**

* Proof of your higher education entrance qualification
* Proof of your Bachelor's degree or a degree recognised as equivalent that opens up access to the Master's degree programme with final degree grade
* Current transcript of records showing the required minimum competences
* Proof of language proficiency

**For applicants who did not obtain their first degree in an EU/EEA member state**

* UniAssist certificate – the so-called ‘Vorprüfungsdokumentation (VPD)’ document. An evaluation report is not sufficient.  
   *If your preferred starting winter semester is not yet available on UniAssist, please select the respective summer semester of the year of your application instead. Further information can be found on the [website of the International Office](https://www.uni-wuerzburg.de/en/international/studying-in-wuerzburg/degree-programmes/application/requesting-a-preliminary-review-vpd-from-uni-assist/) or on the [webiste of UniAssist](https://www.uni-assist.de/en/tools/uni-assist-universities/detail/hochschule/568/).*
* Curriculum vitae
* Applicants with a first degree from Vietnam/China/India: APS certificate
* Document and information on your higher education entrance qualification
* Proof of a Bachelor's degree or a degree recognised as equivalent that opens up access to the Master's degree programme with final degree grade  
   *If no certificate is available yet: at least 150 ECTS with a grade point average of 2.5 or better (for the Management, International Economic Policy and Information Systems degree programmes) or better than 2.0 (for the Management International degree programme)*

### Submit your application

Start your application process online via the [WueStudy](https://wuestudy.zv.uni-wuerzburg.de/qisserver/pages/cs/sys/portal/subMenu.faces?navigationPosition=hisinoneapp_studi_no_login) application portal.

* Start the process by opening an application on the portal.
* Provide proof of minimum competence by **15 July** (via your final certificate or an overview of your grades).
* Upload all **required study documents** and **proof of language proficiency**.
* Submit your online application once you have provided **all required information**.

**Please make sure you spell your name correctly in your application. Otherwise there may be difficulties with your visa application. Once you have been admitted, your name cannot be changed by the university!**

### FAQs

#### I do not fulfill all requirements by the application deadline.

All requirements must be fulfilled at the time of application, including the VPD from UniAssist, overall grade, minimum competencies, and language certificate. If any component is not correctly verified, admission cannot be granted. Missing documentation cannot be submitted later, and preliminary admission is not possible.

#### What is the mentioned VPD from UniAssist and how can i get it.

For admission to Management International every applicant with a non-EU bachelor's degree needs a VPD. Our International Office has a specific website for this context, you find further information [here](https://www.uni-wuerzburg.de/en/international/studying-in-wuerzburg/degree-programmes/application/requesting-a-preliminary-review-vpd-from-uni-assist/).  
 Here you can find a [checklist](https://www.uni-assist.de/fileadmin/Downloads/Tools/Checklisten/EN/UA-Checkliste-VPD-Verfahren-EN.pdf), how to request the VPD at UniAssist.

#### I have requested the VPD from UniAssist, but it is still being processed.

Admission cannot be granted until the final VPD has been issued. The evaluation report is not valid and does not replace the final VPD.

#### I have a VPD for another university. Will this be recognized in my application?

A VPD issued for another university cannot be used for admission to the University of Würzburg. You must request a new VPD from UniAssist specifically for the University of Würzburg. Admission is not possible without the correct VPD.

How to enroll after admission
-----------------------------

### Documents for enrolment

For enrolment **without an EU university degree**, you must submit the **following documents digitally**:

* Application for enrollment
* Higher education entrance qualification (scan of a certified copy)
* German Health insurance certificate
* Proof of payment / payment receipt for the study fees - [more information](https://www.uni-wuerzburg.de/en/studying-at-jmu/studienangelegenheiten/beitraegeundgebuehren/)
* Scan of ID card / passport
* Curriculum vitae
* Proof of previous academic progress (transcript of records)
* Admission letter
* Scan of the visa --> Can be submitted via e-mail ([international.students@uni-wuerzburg.de)](mailto:international.students@uni-wuerzburg.de)
* If already available: Graduation certificate (scan of a certified copy) --> Can be submitted later until January 2026 to the Department 2.2 of the University

For further information about required documents for non-EU citizens, please contact the [International Office](https://www.uni-wuerzburg.de/international/): [international@uni-wuerzburg.de](mailto:international@uni-wuerzburg.de). They will provide you with information on which further documents we require.

### FAQs

#### I have been admitted. What should I do next?

With your admission letter you can register for your visa at your local embassy. Please register early, because the process needs some time. In the meantime, please get a german health insurance and start lookling after a residence in Würzburg.

#### I have been admitted, but I am not yet enrolled in the Master Management International program. What should I do?

Please contact the International Office ([eMail](mailto:international.students@uni-wuerzburg.de "eMail International Office")) of the University of Würzburg. The enrollment is done by the Internatinal Office and the colleagues are happy to answer your questions about enrollment.

#### I will receive my visa after official start of the lectures. What should I do?

If you are already enrolled, no further action is required. All our lectures are without compulsory attendance. Just come as soon as you have your visa and are on site. In the meantime, we recommend that you catch up on all missed lectures. Select your courses on [WueStudy](https://wuestudy.zv.uni-wuerzburg.de/qisserver/pages/cs/sys/portal/hisinoneStartPage.faces "WueStudy") and look at the lecture contents on [WueCampus](https://wuecampus.uni-wuerzburg.de/moodle/?lang=en "WueCampus").

#### I am an enrolled student of the Master Management International program. How can I connect with other students in the program?

Each of our master’s programs has its own WhatsApp community group. To join, please contact us via [eMail](mailto:master@wiwi.uni-wuerzburg.de "eMail").
