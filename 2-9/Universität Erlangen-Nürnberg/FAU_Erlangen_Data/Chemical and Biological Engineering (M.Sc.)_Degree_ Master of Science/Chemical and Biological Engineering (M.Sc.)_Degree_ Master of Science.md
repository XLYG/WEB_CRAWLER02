### What is the degree program about?

Chemical and Biological Engineering is a discipline of engineering that deals with the modification of substances through chemical, physical and biological processes. Acting as a link between chemistry, biotechnology, mechanical engineering and construction, it is the task of chemical and bioengineers to implement the processes developed on a laboratory scale on a production scale.

The degree program lasts for four semesters and builds on the knowledge and skills acquired in the Bachelor’s degree program. Students of the degree program learn advanced scientific methods and gain more in-depth knowledge of chemical and biological process engineering.

### Design and structure

The degree program allows students to choose their own areas of specialization and includes elective modules and supplementary modules, a three-week project development course, a twelve-week work placement in industry and a Master’s thesis which is completed over six months.

Students can choose four specializations out of the seven core subjects to match their interests and career aspirations.

A choice of elective modules on a wide range of current, research-oriented topics – of which students select four – allows students to choose their own focus areas. The catalog of elective modules is updated at the beginning of each semester. Students are also required to take four different supplementary modules.

Students can choose from the following **specialization modules**:

* Bioreaction and bioprocess engineering
* Chemical reaction engineering
* Fluid mechanics
* Mechanical process engineering
* Separation science and technology
* Simulation of granular and molecular systems
* Technical thermodynamics

Students can choose from **elective modules** such as:

* Chemische Energiespeicherung (Chemical energy storage)
* Clean combustion technologies
* Fuel cells and electrolyzers
* Maschinelles Lernen und Künstliche Intelligenz im Ingenieurwesen (Machine learning and artificial intelligence in engineering)
* Self-organization processes
* Process technologies
* Umweltbioverfahrenstechnik (Environmental bioprocess engineering)

The curriculum includes also two elective modules that can be chosen from the offerings of the Faculty of Sciences or Faculty of Engineering or from the offerings to all students at FAU (e.g. key qualifications or language courses). More information is available in the [module handbook](https://www.cbi.studium.fau.de/studierende/pruefungsordnungen/#collapse_0/) and [list of elective modules](https://www.cbi.studium.fau.de/studierende/master/).120 ECTS credits are required to successfully complete the Master’s degree program.

### Fields of study and specializations

It is possible to set an individual focus by choosing specialization modules and elective modules or get a broad education in the most important areas of chemical and biological engineering.

### Which qualities and skills do I need?

* Interest in solving engineering problems
* Interest in getting to know the world and its mathematical and physical relationships
* Enjoy working in a team, but also have the ability to work independently (self-organization)
* Willingness to help solve interdisciplinary tasks
* Enjoyment and curiosity about scientific experiments

### How to apply

### Applicants from non-EU countries (application fee and maximum number of applications)

**Processing fee of 100 euros per application**

As of winter semester 2026/27, FAU will be charging a processing fee of 100 euros per application for applicants from Non-EU states. Further information is available on the website [Fees for applications from non-EU states](https://www.fau.eu/studying/international-students/application-and-enrollment-for-international-applicants/fees-for-applications-from-non-eu-states/).

**Maximum number of applications**

Applicants from non-EU countries who do not have a German university entrance qualification cannot submit more than **three applications**. Withdrawn applications also count if they were withdrawn while they had the status “Being processed”, “Valid” or “Admissions offer currently not possible”.

We would therefore recommend considering carefully which degree programs you would like to apply for before actually applying. After you have submitted three applications, you cannot submit another one.

Non-EU countries are all the states that are neither a member state of the European Union, nor a contracting state for the Agreement on the European Economic Area, nor or a contracting state of the Schengen Agreement.

## Validity of the admissions letter

### Renewed application after being offered a place for a previous semester

The admissions letter will remain valid provided no significant changes have been made either to the Master’s degree program or to the requirements for admission to the Master’s degree program.

If applicants do not enroll in the semester of admission, they may re-apply in a later semester.

Applicants for a Master’s degree program who applied for and were offered a place on the same Master’s degree program in the previous semester must still apply online via the [campo](http://www.campo.fau.de) application portal. In addition to their application documents, they must also upload their admissions letter from the previous semester in the upload section.
