### What is the degree program about?

The consecutive master’s degree program in mechanical engineering with a standard period of study of four semesters builds on the bachelor’s degree program. The aim of the master’s degree program is to provide you with in-depth engineering methods and knowledge as well as research-qualifying scientific working methods. The following fields of study are available for selection in the master’s degree program:

* General Mechanical Engineering (AMB)
* Manufacturing Technology (FT)
* Computer-aided product development (RPE)
* International Production Engineering and Management (IP)

### Design and structure

The master’s degree program is open to above-average bachelor’s and diploma (FH) graduates and can be completed as a full-time program in 4 semesters or as a part-time program in 8 semesters.

In the first 3 (part-time: 6) semesters, students acquire competencies in various elective subjects in mechanical engineering and related engineering disciplines, taking into account current research results, of which they specialize in two areas. This also includes the preparation of a scientific  
project with a presentation. There are four fields of study to choose from.

The 4th semester (part-time: 7th-8th) includes the Master’s thesis, with which the students prove that they can work on a scientific problem independently and at the highest scientific level (ABMPO/TechFak § 32). Practical relevance and „employability“ are also ensured by a compulsory practical work experience.

#### Content related master requirements

The basic prerequisite for admission to a Master’s degree program is an appropriately qualifying good Bachelor’s degree (minimum grade 2.5). If the final grade is >2.5, the applicant will be invited to an oral entrance examination. This lasts approx. 15 minutes.

Applicants will be evaluated on the following criteria:

* Secure knowledge in the subject-specific fundamentals
* Good knowledge in the field of a subject-specific specialization corresponding to a selectable field of study of the master’s program
* Description of a relevant subject-related project
* Knowledge of the relevant literature
* Positive prognosis based on increasing performance in the course of studies to date

Consecutive degree program /Specialized bachelor’s degree:

* Mechanical Engineering

Subject-related bachelor’s degree / access to master’s possible with conditions:

* Vocational Education Technology: if field of study or technical application subject Mechanical Engineering chosen in Bachelor’s degree.
* International Production Engineering and Management
* Mechatronics
* Medical technology: if mechanical engineering as a field of study or technical application was selected in the bachelor’s degree program
* Industrial Engineering and Management: if Mechanical Engineering as a field of study or technical application was selected in the Bachelor’s degree program

**Application**

* Diploma Supplement or complete overview of subjects and grades (Academic Report)

* Letter of motivation/application (please prepare yourself with date and original signature, no pre-printed form available)
