### What is the degree program about?

The systems of teacher education in Germany vary widely. Many federal states have switched to a tiered study structure (Bachelor/Master of Education). In Bavaria, the teacher training program for Gymnasium is modular, but concludes with the First State Examination, which is the admission requirement for the preparatory service in Bavaria.

At FAU Erlangen-Nuremberg, you have the option of earning a Master of Education in addition to or as an alternative to the First State Examination. The Master of Education is an internationally recognized university degree. It opens up additional professional and academic perspectives at home and abroad.

### Design and structure

The teaching-related Master’s degree program Gymnasium comprises 120 credit points (ECTS) and usually lasts 4 semesters. The teaching-related Master’s degree program Gymnasium is related to the teacher training program Gymnasium in such a way that the first three semesters of the Master’s program represent the modules of the 7th, 8th and 9th semester of the Bavarian teacher training program for Gymnasium. This study structure makes it possible for credit points earned in the Gymnasium teacher training program to be credited in the Master’s program. Thus, a maximum of 90 credit points can be credited.

The 4th master’s semester comprises 30 credit points. The master’s thesis is written in this semester. The master’s thesis can be written in all three areas of the teacher training program, i.e. in subject-specific science, subject-specific didactics or educational science. For this purpose, you must independently seek a topic and the appropriate reviewer. Early arrangements are therefore necessary.

#### Content related master requirements

For the master’s program, a teacher training degree from a university at bachelor’s level for the teaching profession Gymnasium with two subjects offered at FAU in the combination with at least six semesters of regular study time or 180 ECTS credits or a comparable degree from a university is required.

In the teacher training program Gymnasium at FAU, a corresponding Bachelor’s certificate can be applied for. This Bachelor’s certificate is mandatory for application to the Master’s program. Students enrolled in the Gymnasium teaching program at FAU can apply for the master’s program at the earliest after obtaining the bachelor’s certificate. An application in a later semester is not problematic.

The Bachelor’s degree should have been completed with an overall grade of at least „good“ (2.5). Applicants with a Bachelor grade up to 3.0 must take an oral entrance examination. Applicants with a Bachelor’s grade worse than 3.0 cannot be admitted to the Master’s program. The application for the teaching-related Master’s program is made via campo
