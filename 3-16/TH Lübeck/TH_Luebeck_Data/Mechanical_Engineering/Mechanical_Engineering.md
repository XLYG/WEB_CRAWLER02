Valerie

Der Masterstudiengang Mechanical Engineering zeichnet sich durch eine breite ingenieurswissenschaftliche Ausrichtung, praxisintegrierte Projekte und eine hohe Flexibilität bei der Wahlfachgestaltung aus. Dabei ist ein besonderes Merkmal die internationale Ausrichtung des Programms.

Aufbauend auf einem Bachelorstudiengang Maschinenbau vertieft und erweitert der Masterstudiengang die bereits erworbenen Kenntnisse und Fähigkeiten im Engineering.

Unsere Vorlesungen und Laborpraktika legen einen besonderen Fokus auf Innovation, Nachhaltigkeit und Digitalisierung. Unser breites Wahlfachangebot erlaubt Ihnen je nach beruflicher Zielsetzung Ihren eigenen Schwerpunkt im Studium zu legen. Dazu trägt auch die Option bei, an unseren Partnerhochschulen im Ausland zu studieren (International Design Engineer).

## Was erwartet Sie bei uns?

Bei uns erwartet Sie ein zukunftsweisendes, anwendungsbezogenes und international ausgerichtetes Studium mit einer sehr guten Betreuung. Sowohl alleine als auch im Team erarbeiten Sie Lösungen für anspruchsvolle Aufgaben.

Eine individuelle Ausrichtung ihres Studiums steht bei uns im Vordergrund. Im Studium Mechanical Engineering können Sie Schwerpunkte entsprechend Ihrer Interessen durch eine individuelle Kombination von Wahlpflichtmodulen setzen.

Ein umfassendes technisches Studium mit hohem Praxisanteil und eine enge Vernetzung mit der Wirtschaft sind bei uns selbstverständlich. Darüber hinaus vermitteln wir wichtige Schlüsselqualifikationen im Bereich Wirtschaft und Management, um Sie bestmöglich auf das Berufsleben vorzubereiten.

Damit Sie in einem global agierenden Arbeitsumfeld sprachsicher auftreten können, werden ab dem ersten Semester alle Lehrveranstaltungen in Englisch angeboten.

# Application and admission information  Master Mechanical Engineering 

## APPLICATION PERIODS 

Summer semester (starting in March):Applicants who graduated in Germany or EU:November 1st  January 15th Applicants with international degrees (Non-EU countries):October 1st - December 1st Winter semester (starting in September):Applicants who graduated in Germany or EU:May 1st  July 15th Applicants with international degrees (Non-EU countries):April 1st  June 1st 

## PREREQUISITES 

## Applicants who graduated in Germany, EU or NON-EU countries:

Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) or Bachelor of Engineering (B.Eng.) in Mechanical Engineering.The previous Bachelor's degree must be equal to 210 credit points (cP) based on the European Credit Transfer System (ECts). The overall grade of the Bachelor's degree must be at least equivalent to a grade of 2.5 in the German grading system to participate in the selection process for admission.

If you hold a Bachelor's degree with 180 CP based on the ECTS, an application is possible with an overall grade of 2.5 or better. In this case, conditional admission may be offered. This can prolong your studies for one semester or more because you have to successfully pass 30 cP from our Bachelor degree in addition. Those classes are either in German or English.



Bachelor's degrees from other fields of engineering can be taken into consideration. In this case a letter of motivation is required (exception THL alumni with Bachelor´s degree Wirtschaftsingenieurwesen,majoring in Mechanical Engineering).



## Important notice to international applicants:

Academic credits gained are not always comparable worldwide. Although having the same name,qualification levels may differ from country to country. Therefore, not all 4-years-bachelor’s programs are considered equivalent to 210 CP based on ECTS.



Uni-assist is THL's partner during the application process. All applicants with international documents have to apply through uni-assist.de.



Uni-assist evaluates an applicant's academic degree according to standards specified by the German Standing Conference of the Ministers of Education and Cultural Affairs (KMK). The standards can be checked atwww.anabin.de(German).



## Special notice to applicants from India and Pakistan:

Past experience has shown that many Bachelor's degrees from India or Pakistan (Bachelor of Engineering or Technology) are not equivalent to 210 CP based on ECTS.
The program is taught in English. The thesis project is carried out in the German industry.English and German language proficiency have to be proven as shown below:

Proof of a good knowledge of English at level B2 according to the Common European Framework of Reference can be made by:

- A test of English as a Foreign Language (TOEFL) with good results 

- Or a comparable internationally recognized English test result that provides evidence of the corresponding language level, e.g. IELTs, Cambridge 

Applicants are exempt from providing an English language test certificate, if they fulfill one of the following criteria:

- A minimum of 5 years continuous language lessons in English during school education 

- At least six months of study or work in an English-speaking country 

- English was the official language of study qualifying for the Mechanical Engineering qualifying degree program 



Proof of a good knowledge of German at level B1 according to the Common European Framework of Reference can be made by:

Internationally acknowledged language test, minimum level B1 based on the CEFR (= Common European Framework for References), e.g. Goethe 

Applicants are exempt from providing an English language test certificate, if they fulfill one of the following criteria:

-German was the official language during school education.

German was the official language of the study program qualifying ,Master of Science Mechanical Engineering'.



Please note: Applicants with a German level A2 based on the CEFR may receive conditional admission and are expected to continue their German language education during their studies until B1 level.

## APPLICATION PROCEDURE 

## Applicants with a German degree 

You are asked to apply directly via our application portal on our website (see here). More information can be found on the above mentioned website. Please click 'Bewerbung Master' and read the information provided (in particular if you have not yet completed your bachelor's degree).

Graduates or future graduates please apply through our application portal.

At least 180 CP and a current grade point average of minimum 2.5 (German grading system) are required for an application. In addition, proof of registration for the bachelor's thesis must be submitted, should this not be evident from the transcript of records (Qis).



Note: THL graduates with Bachelor's degrees from other fields of engineering have to submit a letter of motivation (exception THL alumni with Bachelor's degree Wirtschaftsingenieurwesen, majoring Mechanical Engineering - please see above).



## Applicants with a degree outside from Germany (EU and Non-EU)

All applicants with international degrees (EU and Non-EU) have to apply via uni-assist's online portal Please check the uni-assist-website to ensure submission of all further necessary application documents.Additional documents might be required based on your country of origin. Please see the information provided by uni-assist for more details.



We participate in the online-only application process. You can upload all the documents for an onlineonly application into your My assist account — even without official certification. Please do not send documents by post to uni-assist. The originals have to be presented at the time of enrollment.

Please note: Uni-assist will process your application only if you pay the handling fee (75 Euro).

Please do not use the university website's portal for German applicants.

As an international applicant it is not possible to apply with an unfinished degree.



## Application for an additional master’s degree 

Applicants with a German Master's degree apply directly via our application portal (see here). Additional requirements:

certificate of regular period of studies 

letter of motivation 

degree certificates

Attachments reguired: All mentioned documents (see above under application documents) and original or official authentication and sworn translation of transcript of records (Bachelor and Master),Bachelor´s degree certificate and module catalogue.

## SELECTION PROCEDURE 

## Applicants with a German or EU-degree:

Notifications of admission will be sent within three weeks after the application deadline to you via our application portal. You can check your current application status in the application portal. You will receive the access data after registration in the application portal.



## Applicants with international degrees (Non-EU countries):

You can check your application status via My.Uni-assist. There they will inform you, if something is missing. Only if this step is completed successfully, your application will be considered in the official selection procedure.



A decision about admission will be sent to you within four weeks after the application deadline to you via application portal.



Please note: Admission is highly competitive. The number of students admitted is limited to 15 students per semester (German AND international students together).



We appreciate your interest and wish you the best of success with your application.
