# Strategy, Global Risk & Security Management (MBA)

---

---

The combination of strategy, risk & security management in a global context forms the core of the MBA. To master the environmental influences and competitive forces as well as the increasing demands on corporate strategy and security, the Strategy, Global Risk & Security Management (MBA) degree provides excellent preparation for the new management requirements in a complex environment. The intercultural exchange between students is particularly facilitated by the worldwide study option in English and in a digital form.

Zulassung und Bewerbung

**Admission requirements**

* For the admission a Diploma / Bachelor degree (210 ECTS) (e.g. in the fields of economics, engineering, humanities or related fields) or an equivalent successful domestic or foreign degree is required.
* At least one year of professional experience in the respective field of study acquired after the first academic degree (i.e. professional experience in the professional environment of corporate strategy, expansion, business development, crisis management, strategic controlling, internal public safety, corporate security, business security, project or corporate management, internationalization as well as in related areas of companies and public institutions.) All areas of management such as HR, controlling, marketing etc. are also accepted.
* Admission requirements with a Bachelor degree with less than 210 ECTS: If you have obtained a Bachelor degree with only 180 ECTS there is a possibility to substitute the missing 30 ECTS credit points with qualified work experience, gained after your first academic degree.

**Application and student advising service**

Before applying please contact your Programme Manager to receive further important information on the study programme. The contact details can be found on this page.

The application for this degree programme takes place [online](https://www.thi.de/en/international/studies/application "Online application website THI").

All relevant information about the application process and documents is provided there. Once your application has been submitted, you can check your applicant status.

Each year, the application period for the summer semesters starts November 15, the application period for the winter semesters starts May 2.

**University entrance qualification acquired abroad**

If the higher education entrance qualification was acquired abroad, a preliminary examination of the higher education entrance qualification by **uni-assist** is required.

Please note that the processing time of uni-assist can take 4 to 6 weeks.

For more information on applying to study from abroad, please visit the [website master application from abroad](https://www.thi.de/en/studies/application/masterapplication-from-abroad/ "website study application from abroad").
