# Global Management (MBA)

The global economy is changing rapidly, from supply networks, to partnerships, to borderless artificial intelligence. Our MBA Global Management develops business leaders who know how to act in these situations and who recognize that one of the best ways to learn is to bring different voices to the table. Students of our MBA programme will learn what it takes to lead ethically and responsibly and how to focus on strategic goals in the long run.

Our MBA Global Management will help you gain a global perspective and provide you with an enriching learning environment for your career development and personal progress. The program conveys in-depth specialist knowledge from the areas of operational and strategic management, such as economics, international sales and financial accounting. You will extend on your professional leadership skills by working in groups and in projects with international venture creation.

## Wissenswertes auf einen Blick

Zulassung und Bewerbung

**Admission requirements**

* Bachelor degree (210 ECTS)
* B2-level English proficiency
* At least one year of relevant work experience after completion of undergraduate studies
* Admission requirements with a Bachelor degree with less than 210 ECTS: If you have obtained a Bachelor degree with only 180 ECTS there is a possibility to substitute the missing 30 ECTS credit points with qualified work experience, gained after your first academic degree

**Application and student advising service**

The application for this degree programme takes place [online](https://www.thi.de/en/international/studies/application "Online application website THI").

All relevant information about the application process and documents is provided there. Once your application has been submitted, you can check your applicant status.

Each year, the application period for the summer semesters starts November 15, the application period for the winter semesters starts May 2.

**University entrance qualification acquired abroad**

If the higher education entrance qualification was acquired abroad, a preliminary examination of the higher education entrance qualification by **uni-assist** is required.

Please note that the processing time of uni-assist can take 4 to 6 weeks.

For more information on applying to study from abroad, please visit the [website master application from abroad](https://www.thi.de/en/studies/application/masterapplication-from-abroad/ "website study application from abroad").

Target Group

* We are focusing on individuals who want to make a difference in leading global business and are not afraid to think out of the box
* We are looking forward to work with people who are technology driven and interested in strengthening their leadership abilities

Job profiles

* You are able to take management positions in an global acting company who develops strong innovative and digital driven business models
* Most of our students will work in the area of Business Development, Product Management, Leading Production Areas, Consulting and many more

Extras

* Our overall goal is to develop leaders who are capable of turning innovation into long term impact
* Our programme takes an interdisciplinary approach to business fundamentals, so you are able to move seamlessly from “status quo” to “how should it be”
* You will be trained to think in different ways of thinking and in multiple perspectives
* You will learn to apply your knowledge as problem solving strategy in complex business situations.
