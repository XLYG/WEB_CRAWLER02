# Master of Engineering (M.Eng.)Network Technology and Grid Operation Gas/Water

The liberalisation of the energy market and the greater competitive challenges which this brings are making it even more important to treat sectors which used to be considered separately in a holistic way. Esslingen University of Applied Sciences has teamed up with Stuttgart University of Applied Sciences and the Netze BW training centre to offer the part-time Master programme in Network Technology and Grid Operation Gas/Water (M.Eng.). The programme was developed in conjunction with the Deutscher Verein des Gas- und Wasserfachs e. V. (DVGW), the VDE (FNN) and major energy suppliers with the aim of preparing practitioners to take on managerial roles in supply companies.

## Content Information

In the first two semesters, students acquire more in-depth knowledge of the gas/water and electrical engineering sectors. This is followed by a common semester which examines overriding topics relating to network and grid management. Students then spend the 4th semester working on their Master thesis.

## Career Perspectives

Network engineers are responsible for planning and setting up the operation and the maintenance of distribution networks. They develop complex engineering-specific solutions which are implemented in networks. And on completing this programme, they have all the qualifications needed to take on key responsibility in network management. These engineers are thus best qualified for a career as a specialist or manager in supply companies operating in all sectors.

## Application/Admission

This degree programme is offered jointly by Esslingen University and Netze BW GmbH. Applications must be submitted via Netze BW.

### This awaits you

The Network Technology and Grid Operation Gas/Water programme has been designed for optimum compatibility between study and work. It provides online independent-study units in conjunction with face-to-face teaching at Netze BW in Esslingen and in the modern labs at Esslingen University.

#### **Exemption from Student Services Organisation subscription**

Disabled students enrolled on the Applied Surface and Material Science programme can be partially exempt from the Student Services Organisation subscription.

When the **severity of your disability**entitles you to use local public transport for free, you can apply to have the portion of the subscription which covers the SemesterTicket reimbursed. Please pay the **full Student Services Organisation subscription** when you enrol/ re-enrol and then submit an informal application for reimbursement to **Studierendenwerk Ulm, James Franck-Ring 8, 89081 Ulm** and include the following information: Copy of your disability ID card, certificate of enrolment, bank statement to prove the semester fees and subscriptions have been paid, details of your bank account (for the money to be reimbursed).

Note: The partial exemption applies only to disabled students on the Applied Surface and Material Science programme, which is offered in cooperation with Aalen University of Applied Sciences in the Ostalb Mobil-Verkehrsverbund public transport network. **The VVS Stuttgart no longer offers this exemption.**
