# Master of Engineering (M.Eng.)Automotive Engineering

Would you like to study vehicles as complete systems within the framework of future-oriented mobility concepts and help build the mobility systems of the future? Then the Master’s degree programme in Automotive Engineering at Esslingen University of Applied Sciences, right in the automotive hotspot of the Stuttgart region, is the right decision for you. You will deepen your skills and later develop sustainable mobility solutions as a specialist or manager.

## Content Information

The Master’s programme offers you a wide range of courses in the field of Automotive Engineering. You will develop knowledge and expertise in the areas of motors, car body development, driver assistance systems and automated driving. You will develop your scientific skills through a research project and your Master’s thesis.

## Career Perspectives

The Master’s degree opens up a wide range of career perspectives. Your potential employer can rely on your professional problem-solving skills. Alternatively, the content of the study programme will enable you to find a future-oriented position as a specialist or manager in research and development. Or you can pursue an academic career and decide to do a doctorate after your Master’s degree.

With your Master’s degree, you will be a sought-after specialist or leader in these areas

* Development and testing of vehicles and vehicle components
* Technical service and sales for vehicle components and complete vehicles
* Quality assurance
* Expert assessor and reporting
* Research

## General Admission Requirements

I obtained my first degree abroad. Can I apply for a master’s programme in Esslingen with my bachelor’s degree / diploma?

Yes, but you have to get your foreign qualification accredited and possibly provide proof of German language knowledge.

#### **Getting your foreign qualifications recognised in germany**

Please apply for accreditation **in time**, as the accreditation certificate has to be included in your application portfolio.

* **German applicants with a foreign bachelor’s degree certificate / diploma** should inquire at the  [Admissions Office](https://web.archive.org/web/20250208170447/https:/www.hs-esslingen.de/en/study/application-and-enrolment/applications-for-a-masters-degree-course) for details.
* **Foreign applicants with a foreign bachelor’s degree certificate / diploma** have to get their certificates accredited by the[University of Constance (Studienkolleg Konstanz)](https://web.archive.org/web/20250208170447/https:/www.htwg-konstanz.de/en/academics/center-for-international-students-konstanz/recognition-of-certificates/procedure). The University checks to see whether your qualifications are comparable to German university entrance qualifications and calculates a grade according to the German grading system.

[To the Studienkolleg Konstanz (foreign applicants)](https://web.archive.org/web/20250208170447/https:/www.htwg-konstanz.de/en/academics/center-for-international-students-konstanz/recognition-of-certificates/procedure)

---

#### **LANGUAGE SKILLS / GERMAN LANGUAGE TEST**

Our courses are taught mainly in **German**. **Foreign nationals with foreign certificates** must therefore provide proof of having passed a German language test, at the latest when they enrol at Esslingen University of Applied Sciences. We accept the following certificates:

* [DSH-Prüfung](https://web.archive.org/web/20250208170447/https:/www.dsh-germany.com/info/#niveaustufen/sprachniveau-b2/) at level 2 or 3
* [TestDaf Examination](https://web.archive.org/web/20250208170447/http:/www.testdaf.de/) (The examination is considered passed with an average of 4.0)
* [Deutsches Sprachdiplom der Kultusministerkonferenz](https://web.archive.org/web/20250208170447/https:/www.kmk.org/themen/deutsches-sprachdiplom-dsd.html)
[(German Language Certificate of the Education Ministers Conference)](https://web.archive.org/web/20250208170447/https:/www.kmk.org/themen/deutsches-sprachdiplom-dsd.html)
* [Language test telc C1 Hochschule](https://web.archive.org/web/20250208170447/https:/www.telc.net/pruefungsteilnehmende/sprachpruefungen/pruefungen/detail/telc-deutsch-c1-hochschule.html)

---

#### **Exemption from Student Services Organisation subscription**

Disabled students enrolled on the Applied Surface and Material Science programme can be partially exempt from the Student Services Organisation subscription.

When the **severity of your disability**entitles you to use local public transport for free, you can apply to have the portion of the subscription which covers the SemesterTicket reimbursed. Please pay the **full Student Services Organisation subscription** when you enrol/ re-enrol and then submit an informal application for reimbursement to **Studierendenwerk Ulm, James Franck-Ring 8, 89081 Ulm** and include the following information: Copy of your disability ID card, certificate of enrolment, bank statement to prove the semester fees and subscriptions have been paid, details of your bank account (for the money to be reimbursed).

Note: The partial exemption applies only to disabled students on the Applied Surface and Material Science programme, which is offered in cooperation with Aalen University of Applied Sciences in the Ostalb Mobil-Verkehrsverbund public transport network. **The VVS Stuttgart no longer offers this exemption.**

#### Fees for **International students**

Students who are **not nationals of a member state of the European Union or another signatory state of the Agreement on the European Economic Area** are fundamentally required to pay tuition fees. The **fees for international students** are 1,500 euros per semester. The law provides for a few **exceptions**, however, whereby international students are exempt from tuition fees. If you fulfil these criteria and provide us with the appropriate documentation to prove this **in due time** before you enrol, you will not have to pay the tuition fees for international students.

**Application form for exemption from payment of tuition fees**

[Form for exemption from payment of tuition fees](https://web.archive.org/web/20250208170447/https:/www.hs-esslingen.de/fileadmin/media/Dokumente/Bewerbung/Internationale_Bewerber/Formular_Befreiung_Studiengebuehrenplicht.pdf "Download Formular_Befreiung_Studiengebuehrenplicht.pdf (1,176 MB) as Pdf")

#### Note on tuition fees

It may be possible to be exempted from the payment of tuition fees during a sabbatical semester, a compulsory practical semester or if you have disabilities.
