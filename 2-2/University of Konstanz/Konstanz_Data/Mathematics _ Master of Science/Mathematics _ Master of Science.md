#### Prospects

#### Career prospects

There is a broad spectrum of work with tasks such as calculating the aerodynamic resistance of a vehicle’s bodywork, or improving the production processes of a software provider. Whilst a bank needs an assessment model for risk management, an insurance company is looking for a market analysis and the evaluation of its own insurance products. By studying mathematics you will acquire the necessary basic methodical qualifications for this, whilst you gain specific specialist knowledge and skills in the various areas of your minor.

In the professional world mathematics in the narrower sense often plays a lesser role, whereas the skills, such as the ability to think in abstract terms, to recognise logical relationships and to formulate models gain in importance. Larger numbers of mathematicians are employed in industry and the commercial sphere, for instance in company development departments and in data processing, in administration departments, insurance companies, banking and the software sector, and in technical and economic service enterprises.

#### Study structure

During your studies you will focus on certain areas of mathematics. You will choose your area of specialisation from the department’s main research areas (analysis and numerics, real geometry and algebra, stochastic and statistics). In addition to this, you will attend courses in non-mathematical disciplines (e.g. biology, chemistry, computer science, physics, psychology, philosophy, linguistics or economics).

### Study requirements

#### Admission requirements for studying

* Successfully completed bachelor's programme in mathematics or a related subject at an institution of higher education or recognised *Berufsakademie* or equivalent institution (abroad).
* Adequate knowledge of mathematics for a successful start and course of studies. Generally, you have to document 120 ECTS credits in completed coursework/exams at the level of the Konstanz bachelor's programme. This also includes in-depth knowledge in one of the areas of specialization of the Konstanz bachelor's programme (see [module guide](https://www.uni-konstanz.de/en/study/while-you-study/study-portals/module-guides/)).
* If you need to catch up on mathematics skills you can take up to 30 ECTS credits within the first year of study. You will then be admitted to the master's programme on the condition that you pass the corresponding exams within the specified period. In this case, we highly recommended you consult with our Student Advisory Service as early as possible.
* International applicants need documentation of their German or English language skills (see tab “Required foreign language skills”)
* Applicants from Shanghai Jiao Tong University for the double-degree programme in the context of the master's programme have to document English language skills as stated in the cooperation agreement.

Further information can be found in the [admission regulations](https://www.uni-konstanz.de/en/study/before-you-study/application-and-enrolment/admission-regulations/).

#### Required language skills

**Foreign language skills**  
 Aside from knowledge of German, you do **not** have to document any **other language skills** for the master's programme Mathematics. However, as large parts of the research literature are written in English, good English skills are highly recommended.

* Applicants from Shanghai Jiao Tong University for the double-degree programme have to document English language skills as stated in the cooperation agreement.

**German language skills**  
 International applicants have to submit [documentation of their German **or** English language skills](https://www.uni-konstanz.de/en/study/before-you-study/application-and-enrolment/required-language-skills/):

* **German language skills** at level **DSH-1/TestDaF 3** or higher.  
   Alternatively, you may submit other recognized language tests or recognized school certificates. The University of Konstanz does not offer preparatory German language courses or recognized German language exams. For this reason, you have to submit corresponding documentation of your German language skills along with your application.
* **English language skills** at **B2 level** (TOEFL 87 internet-based or IELTS score 5.5 or Cambridge First Certificate - Grade C).  
   Alternatively you can document:
  + at least four completed courses taught in English in a study programme in the European Economic Area or Switzerland, or
  + a secondary school degree or bachelor’s degree acquired at an English-language educational institution in one of the following countries, or at least one semester abroad spent there: USA, Canada, Great Britain, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, Germany, Austria, Switzerland

In particularly justified cases, the required language skills can also be demonstrated in an interview with a professor whose main employment is in the Department of Mathematics. Such decisions are taken by the Examination Board Mathematics.

### Application

#### Application and application documents

#### Application documents

Please attach the following documents to your application:

* Documentation of your academic degree including details on course work. If you have not received your degree certificate by the end of the application deadline, please attach documentation of all your examination results so far.
* Documentation of 120 ECTS in mathematics, including the following areas of specialization: analysis and numerics, geometry and algebra, statistics, stochastics or differential geometry.
* Current certificate of enrolment or proof of exmatriculation, if applicable (if you have studied at a German university, please indicate your 'Fachsemester’ and ‘Hochschulsemester’).

Important note: Please note the information provided under the tab "Admission requirements".

**International applicants:**

* If your documents are not available in German or English, you will need to have them translated. Please submit a German or English translation of your documents along with the original versions.
* Documentation of German language skills (level DSH-1/TestDaF 3) **or** English language skills (level B2 (TOEFL 87 internet-based, or IELTS score 5.5, or Cambridge First Certificate - Grade C)).
* Applicants from Shanghai Jiao Tong University for the double-degree programme: Documentation of English language skills as stated in the cooperation agreement

An application is possible during the following period(s): 07.05. - 15.09. | 25.11. - 15.03.  

There may be other deadlines if you are applying for admission to a higher semester (for instance, if you are changing subjects or universities).
