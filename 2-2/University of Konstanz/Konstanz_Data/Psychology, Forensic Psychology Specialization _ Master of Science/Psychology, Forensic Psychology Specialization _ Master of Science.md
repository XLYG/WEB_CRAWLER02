Continuing education master's programme for working professionals. You will develop your expertise in diagnosing forensically relevant psychiatric disorders and acquire a basic knowledge of prison psychiatry and psychopharmacology.

You will gain a thorough knowledge of forensic-psychological methods for making risk assessments, with a focus on current research and development. This includes assessing the risk of relapses amongst violent offenders and sex offenders, assessing the danger of actually committing a threatened violent offence, plus developing a subsequent threat management strategy. You will also gain practice-oriented basic skills in preparing criminal responsibility and plausibility expertises. Individual and group therapy skills for offence-oriented interventions are developed in outpatient and in stationary settings.

Our continuing education master's programme Psychology, Forensic Psychology Specialization" is organised by the [Akademie für Wissenschaftliche Weiterbildung (AWW)](https://afww.uni-konstanz.de/de/forensische-psychologie), the University of Konstanz's continuing education institution.

#### Study structure

Students need to acquire in-course academic performance assessment in **4 modules** (each 8 ECTS-credits) and write a **masters’ thesis** (28 ECTS-credits). The programme is structured in such a way that it is possible to write the masters’ thesis parallel to attending courses and continuing professional commitments.

All of the teaching takes place in block seminars (Friday through Sunday), so that you can complete your studies alongside your professional work. You will find more information about the contents of the individual modules on the [websites of the department](https://www.psychologie.uni-konstanz.de/en/forensic-psychology-research-team/study-programme/continuing-education-master-s-programme/).

### Study requirements

#### Admission requirements for studying

To be admitted to this programme you need:

* a degree from a four-year bachelor's programme in psychology or
* a completed master’s programme in psychology or
* a completed 'Facharztausbildung' (specialist doctor training) or equivalent training abroad or
* a completed 'erstes Juristisches Staatsexamen' (state examination in law) with a study concentration in criminology or
* an equivalent degree, also from abroad
* at least one year of professional activity in the area of clinical or forensic psychology or in the field of medicine, jurisprudence or comparable scientific disciplines qualifying for the postgraduate study programme

**Tuition fees**  
 Tuition fees are € 2,500 per semester. Since the standard period of study is four semesters, the total programme costs amount to € 10,000.

Further information can be found in the [admission regulations](https://www.uni-konstanz.de/en/study/before-you-study/application-and-enrolment/admission-regulations/).

#### Fees

**Semester fee**  
 Enrolled students in the Master's Programme Psychology with a Concentration in Forensics must pay a special tuition fee of **2,500 euros per semester.** This is in addition to the other [required fees](https://www.uni-konstanz.de/en/study/study-essentials/finances/other-fees/general-overview/). For the standard study period of 4 semesters, the total amount due is 10,000 euros. If the standard period of study is exceeded, no further tuition fees will be charged.

More information can be found [here](https://www.uni-konstanz.de/en/study/study-essentials/finances/tuition-fees/vocational-master-s-programme-psychology-with-a-concentration-in-forensics/).
