# Agricultural Economics and Social Sciences

---

Agricultural Economics and Agricultural Social Sciences is studied as a Minor Professional Discipline in combination with the Major Professional Discipline Agricultural Sciences in the Master of Education degree program for vocational schools.

The teaching degree for vocational schools combines a professional discipline with [a school subject](https://www.uni-bonn.de/teaching-degree#voc-schools) and educational sciences. The practical semester is a key component of the degree program.

For further information, please refer to the website of the Major Professional Discipline Agricultural Sciences (MEd).

**Possible lines of work:**

Teaching at schools

Please find additional possible lines of work under the degree program [Agricultural and Food Economics (MSc)](https://www.uni-bonn.de/en/studying/degree-programs/degree-programs-a-z/agricultural-and-food-economics-msc).
