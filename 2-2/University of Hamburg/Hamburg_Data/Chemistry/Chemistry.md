### [Beschreibung des Studiengangs](javascript:void(0) "Inhalt verbergen")

The master’s program in Chemistry at the University of Hamburg is a two-year, research-oriented program taught in English. It is designed to provide students with comprehensive theoretical knowledge and practical skills across all major areas of chemistry, concluding in an independent research project.

### [Berufliche Perspektiven](javascript:void(0) "Inhalt anzeigen")

Graduates of the Master of Science in Chemistry program at the University of Hamburg benefit from excellent career prospects in the chemical industries, research sectors (whether academic or non-university research institutes), analytics, and public service. They also find opportunities in related fields, such as environmental engineering, materials science and the automotive and electronics industries. Their roles can involve research and development (R&D), production, quality management, consulting, and management. Their activities may include laboratory work, project management, and corporate consulting. Additionally, the degree provides an opportunity to pursue a Ph.D., which can open doors to higher leadership positions.

### [Studienaufbau](javascript:void(0) "Inhalt anzeigen")

The program begins with an introductory phase lasting one semester, during which the students receive extensive instruction in inorganic, organic, physical, macromolecular and technical, theoretical chemistry, as well as spectroscopy. These lectures aim to establish a strong conceptual understanding.

Complementing the lectures, students participate in an advanced laboratory course focusing on inorganic, organic, physical, as well as macromolecular and technical chemistry, which comprises more than 250 hours of hands-on lab work. This laboratory work takes place during the first and second semesters.

Following the introductory phase, students enter the advanced phase of the program. During this period, they have the opportunity to deepen their knowledge by selecting research-oriented modules covering a broad spectrum of chemistry-related topics. These modules enable students to tailor their studies according to their individual interests and career goals, reflecting current trends and developments in the field. The program concludes with a six-month master's thesis, during which the students undertake an independent research project in a subject area of their choice. This research work provides valuable experience in scientific investigation and academic writing, preparing students for future research or professional careers in chemistry.

Prospective students seeking admission to the master's program in Chemistry are expected to demonstrate a comprehensive understanding of the discipline, with particular emphasis on inorganic, organic, and physical chemistry. This encompasses both theoretical knowledge and practical experience in laboratory work. Moreover, applicants need to have very good command over the English language in order to follow lectures and exams. Applicants to the master’s program must meet the following criteria:

1. A degree in the Bachelor's program in Chemistry at the University of Hamburg or a comparable program at another university.
2. Passing the subject-specific online test.
3. English language proficiency.
