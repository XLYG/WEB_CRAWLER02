### [Beschreibung des Studiengangs](javascript:void(0) "Inhalt verbergen")

Im Mittelpunkt des Master of Innovation, Business and Sustainability (MIBAS) steht der Aufbau von spezifischen Kenntnissen und Kompetenzen in den Bereichen der internationalen Unternehmensführung und der Nachhaltigkeit. Im MIBAS kommen Studierende aus unterschiedlichen Kulturkreisen zusammen und entwickeln gemeinsam ein Verständnis für die komplexen Zusammenhänge in der globalen Wirtschaft. Das Lehrprogramm konfrontiert sie dabei mit den umfangreichen theoretischen und praktischen Herausforderungen, die eine nachhaltige Wirtschaftsweise für Unternehmen und Gesellschaft mit sich bringt.

Im Verlauf des Masterprogramms werden die Studierenden insbesondere für die ethischen, ökologischen, sozialen und politischen Implikationen unternehmerischer Wertschöpfungsprozesse sensibilisiert. Dadurch sollen die Studierenden als künftige Führungskräfte ein besonderes Verantwortungsbewusstsein entwickeln. Es handelt sich um einen internationalen und interdisziplinären Studiengang, der (ab WS 2016/17) komplett auf Englisch unterrichtet wird.

### [Berufliche Perspektiven](javascript:void(0) "Inhalt anzeigen")

Die mit dem MIBAS erworbenen fundierten theoretischen und methodischen Kenntnisse ermöglichen sowohl eine Berufstätigkeit in multinationalen Unternehmen und Organisationen als auch – überdurchschnittliche Leistungen vorausgesetzt – den Einstieg in eine wissenschaftlicheKarriere über die Berechtigung zur Promotion.

### [Studienaufbau](javascript:void(0) "Inhalt anzeigen")

Das zweijährige Masterprogramm ist in sechs thematische Bereiche sowie die abschließende Masterarbeit untergliedert:

• Fundamentals of Innovation, Business and Sustainability (1. Semester)

• Research Methods (1., 2. und 3. Semester)

• Innovation, Sustainability and Knowledge Exchange in Management (2. und 3. Semester)

• Sustainability in Management (2. und 3. Semester)

• Sustainable Finance and Accounting (2. und 3. Semester)

• Electives (2. und 3. Semester)

• Masterarbeit (4. Semester)

## Introduction

The MIBAS is an English taught, two-year degree program that prepares you for challenging positions in multinational companies, international organizations, labor unions, and academia. Those who achieve above-average grades on the program have the opportunity to continue their studies at doctoral level, the prerequisite for a career in academia.

## Admission Requirements

## University degree

A bachelor's degree (at least 180 ECTS) in Socioeconomics or in a comparable interdisciplinary socio-economic degree program, Business Administration, Law, Social Sciences, Sociology, Economics or Business Studies. In exceptional cases, graduates of other academic degree programs may also be admitted, provided that they can usefully build on their knowledge from their previous studies. If you're currently completing the last semester of your bachelor's program, you can still apply and receive conditional acceptance. See our website for details on applying while completing your final bachelor's semester: www.wiso.uni-hamburg.de/en/studienbuerosozialoekonomie/studiengaenge/msc-mibas/entry-requirements.html

## Study aptitude test

A proof of completion of one of the following study aptitude tests:

- Graduate Management Admission Test (GMAT) Focus Edition with a total score of at least 545 points, or
- Graduate Management Admission Test (GMAT) 10th Edition with a total score of at least 570 points, or
- Graduate Record Examination (GRE) General Test with a scaled score of at least 151 points in Verbal Reasoning and a scaled score of at least 156 points in Quantitative Reasoning,or

## Selection criteria

After the application deadline, if the number of applicants exceeds the number of places available, a selection procedure will be necessary:

Applicants are divided into two groups:

- Domestic students ('Bildungsinländer'): German citizens as well as applicants without German citizenship who have obtained their higher education entrance qualification (e.g. Abitur) in Germany.
- Foreign students ('Bildungsausländer'): all non -German applicants who have obtained their higher education entrance qualification (e.g. USA: high school diploma, Pakistan: Higher Secondary (School) Certificate, etc.) outside of Germany.

Each group is allocated 50% of the available places on the program. Applicants are ranked within their group according to the following criteria:

- Final grade achieved in former degree: Applicants are classified within their group according to the grade of the former university degree or according to the current average grade, provided that the final certificate of the former university degree is not yet available at the time of application.
- Test result or lottery procedure: Only if the number of applicants exceeds the number of study places available and several applicants have the same final grade with which they could still obtain a study place, then the result of the study aptitude test will decide between these applicants. In this case, the following ration will be calculated: ratio of the points achieved in the study aptitude test by the applicant to the maximum points which can be achieved in the study aptitude test.

## Examples:

- GMAT: If your total score of your GMAT Focus Edition is 595 points, then your ratio is calculated as follows: 595 ÷ 805 = 0,7391 (i.e. 73,91%). If your total score of your GMAT 10th Edition is 595 points, then your ratio is calculated as follows: 595 ÷ 800 = 0,7437 (i.e. 74,37%).
- GRE: If your score for Verbal Reasoning is 163 points and your score for Quantitative Reasoning is 165 points, then your ratio is calculated as follows: (163 ÷ 170 = 0,9588) + (165 ÷ 170 = 0,9705) = 1,9293 ÷ 2 = 0,9646 (i.e. 96,46%).
- GTEBS: If your achieved test value (standard value) is 102 points, then your ratio is calculated as follows: 102 ÷ 130 = 0,7846 (i.e. 78,46%).

If the result of the study aptitude test of the aforementioned applicants (with the same final grade) is also the same, the decision is made by a lottery procedure for study places.

The top students in each group are then allotted the available places for that year (e.g. if there were 100 total places available, each group would receive 50 places, which would then be allotted to the top 50 applicants in that group).
