# Faculty of Philosophy Assyriology – Bachelor 25%

The Assyriology (Ancient Near East Studies) degree programme focusses on the Sumerian, Babylonian, Assyrian and Hittite cultures in their entire impressive diversity.

## Course Content

In the Bachelor’s degree programme, students will learn the fundamentals of the Akkadian language and the highly-complex cuneiform scripts and gain an overview of the history and cultural history of the ancient Near East. In pursuing the degree programme as a minor subject, students are required, within a relatively short period of time, to master the basics of the ancient Near East Akkadian language and to learn the cuneiform script in its various forms.

## Course Structure

This programme may be combined as a 25% minor with a 75% major in another subject.
