## Admission Requirements

For admittance to the master’s program applicants need to fulfill the following admission requirements:

* Applicants must have successfully completed a degree course, preferably in law, at a German or non-German university.
* Participants must prove a minimum of one year postgraduate work experience in addition to their undergraduate or first degree.
* English language proficiency (of at least C1 CEFR)

In addition to the admission requirements, a letter of motivation and a potential interview will be considered.

For more information, please see the [admissions regulations](/studium/docs/DOC/Zugangssatzungen-Englisch/ZugS-European-Law_uebersetzt.pdf "admissions regulations"). (Please note that the English version of these regulations is for information purposes. Only the German version is legally binding.)

Further information about admission requirements can be found on the website of the program.

## Subject

The MBL-FU is a postgraduate master program in law (LL.M.). It lasts for one academic year. Students obtain in-depth knowledge on cutting-edge questions of European and International Competition and Regulatory Law.  
The program intends to give young jurists and economists from businesses, companies, law firms, and public authorities the opportunity to gain in-depth, specialist knowledge in business and competition law as well as in different regulated markets to enhance their professional skills.   
  
The lecturers are renowned legal and economic academics as well as practitioners from all around the world – from officials of the European Commission and national authorities to attorneys at international law firms and multinational companies. Their classes cover a wide range of topics, from foundational theoretical and economic questions to all aspects of European and international competition law, to intellectual property and other crucial issues driving important sectors of today’s (digital) economy.  
  
Students study seven modules and write a master thesis. Each module consists of in-class lectures, seminars and tutorials. Classes are held on Fridays and Saturdays and occasionally on Thursdays at the Law Department. Online study and work components complement and build upon these lectures. The language of instruction is English.

These regulations govern admissions to the study program in accordance with Section 10.5.2 BerlHG and the selection procedure for the allocation of places in accordance with Section 16 BerlHZG for the master's  degree  program  European  and  International  Business,  Competition,  and  Regulatory  Law within the Department of Law at Freie Universität Berlin.

## Section 2 Number of Available Places and the Application Deadline

- (1) The number of places available for the master's degree program for each given admissions cycle is stipulated in Freie Universität Berlin's General Admission Regulations.
- (2)  The application for admission must be submitted electronically to the Executive Board of Freie Universität Berlin - Admissions Office using the online application system.
- (3) The application deadline for enrolling in the upcoming winter semester is March 15 of each year.
- (4) If the number of applications received is less than the number of available places, the remaining places will be awarded to applicants with a first university degree and subsequent qualified practical professional experience of generally not less than one year. ## Section 3 Selection Criteria

- (1) The following criteria are taken into account in the selection process:
1. Completion of a first university degree, preferably one from a degree program in a legal field,  particularly  if  this  degree  was  obtained  at  an  institute  of  higher  education  outside Germany; completion of the first state examination in law ( erste juristische Staatsprüfung ); or completion of the first examination in law ( erste juristische Prüfung ) in accordance with the German Judiciary Act ( Deutsches Richtergesetz , DRiG).
2.  Proof  of  English  language  skills  at  a  level  equivalent  to  C1  of  the  Common  European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) for applicants who did not complete a university degree at an institution where coursework was conducted in English.
3. Short personal statement (max. three pages) explaining why the applicant wishes to apply for the master's degree program.
4. Information regarding the applicants' prior areas of academic specialization and planned areas of specialization for their master's thesis.
5. Proof of qualified practical professional experience related to the master's degree program, preferably in the fields of competition and regulatory law, of generally not less than one year, following the applicant's first university degree.
- (2) Where necessary, the admissions officers may invite applicants to a selection interview.

## Section 4 Selection Interview

- (1)  The  selection  interview  is  carried  out  by  the  admissions  officers  in  accordance  with  Section  5. Selection interviews are not open to the public and last about 20 minutes per applicant.
- (2) The admissions officers will invite applicants to the selection interviews in writing, providing them with the time and location of their interview. The invitation must be issued and sent to the candidate at least 10 working days before the interview is to take place.
- (3) A record is to be kept of each selection interview evaluating the candidate and the reasons for this evaluation.

## Section 5 Admissions Officers

- (1) At least two admissions officers will be appointed to conduct the selection process. The appointed admissions officers must be members of academic staff involved in carrying out the master's degree program and must be university employees with Freie Universität Berlin as their main employer. The dean of the Department of Law appoints these two people under the authority of Freie Universität Berlin's Executive Board. The admissions officers hold their positions for one admission cycle. These positions may not be filled by substitutes or alternates.
- (2)  The  admissions  officers  suggest  candidates  for  admission  to  the  Executive  Board  -  Admissions Office based on the applicants' suitability and motivation.
- (3) If potentially suitable candidates fail to submit complete applications or if the admissions officers require additional material in order to make a decision, they may request that applicants provide this information by a certain deadline.

## Section 6 Admission Decision

- (1)  The  decision  on  which  applicants  are  to  be  selected  is  made  by  the  Executive  Board  of  Freie Universität Berlin - Admissions Office on the basis of how candidates have been ranked.
- (2) The selected candidates receive a formal notification of admission stating the deadlines for their written  acceptance  and  enrollment.  Failure  to  meet  these  deadlines  will  result  in  the  place  being offered to another candidate.
- (3)  Applicants  who  are  not  admitted  will  receive  a  rejection  letter  that  states  the  reasons  for  the decision.
- (4) To enroll, students must provide evidence that they have paid the amount specified in the fee regulations for the master's degree program.
- (5) The documents submitted for the selection procedure are to be kept until the decision is final; in the case of legal proceedings, they are to be kept until a legally binding decision has been made.

## Section 7 Entry into Force

- (1) These regulations enter into force on the day following their publication in FU-Mitteilungen (the official bulletin of Freie Universität Berlin).
- (2) The admissions regulations for the master's degree program from April 25, 2012 ( FU-Mitteilungen 37/2012, p. 580), therefore lapse when the new regulations come into force.
