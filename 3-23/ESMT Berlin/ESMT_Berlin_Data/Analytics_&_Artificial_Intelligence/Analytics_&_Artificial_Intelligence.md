# Analytics & Artificial Intelligence

## Lead at the intersection of data & business with a master’s in analytics and AI

**The Master in Analytics and Artificial Intelligence (MAAI) prepares you to bridge the worlds of business, data, and AI to improve strategic decision making.** You will work on real business challenges that sharpen your analytical thinking, strengthen your technical fluency, and develop your ability to turn insights into strategic action.

Rather than mastering tools and models, you will explore how AI and data influence organizations and society. You will learn to **evaluate bias, transparency, and accountability** in data-oriented decisions and to apply analytics responsibly in real business contexts.

With the widest range of electives across all ESMT master programs, the MAAI lets you **design a learning journey that reflects your interests**, **connects disciplines**, and **keeps you adaptable** as technology and industries evolve.

### Program highlights

* **A future-focused curriculum** that blends analytical proficiency in areas like data management, machine learning, and decision science with real business applications.
* **Immersion in industry-relevant tools**, platforms, and frameworks such as Python, R, SQL, Tableau, and cloud technologies, and the opportunity for additional self-guided certificates from Amazon, Bloomberg, DataCamp, and more.
* **Exposure to modern analytical methods and frameworks** used across industries, helping you understand their logic, application, and limitations.
* **Customizable and interdisciplinary learning** with the widest range of electives across all MSc programs, in diverse business areas such as global management, innovation and entrepreneurship, and an optional [Finance & Investment Certificate](https://esmt.berlin/degrees/esmt-finance-certificate).
* **Training in the people skills needed by data/AI managers** and analytical advisors, taught via dedicated Negotiation and Communication Clinics, skills seminars, and a Corporate Impact Lab run by top-level business leaders.
* **Built-in opportunities for hands-on application**, including 3- to 6-month [internships](https://esmt.berlin/master-programs/master-analytics-artificial-intelligence/internships-projects "Internships & projects "), study abroad at [20+ partner schools](https://esmt.berlin/master-programs/master-global-management/international-options), [analytics consulting projects](https://esmt.berlin/master-programs/master-analytics-artificial-intelligence/analytics-consulting-project "Analytics Student Project"), and research track with ESMT faculty.
* **World-class** [**faculty**](https://esmt.berlin/master-programs/master-program-faculty) advancing research in analytics and AI, while teaching across degree and executive programs and advising global companies.

### Why choose a master’s in analytics and AI at ESMT Berlin?

* **Develop an analytical mindset** and learn how to identify opportunities, ask sharper questions, and make informed business decisions.
* **Design a learning journey that grows with you**, combining electives across disciplines and exploring what inspires you most.
* **Build awareness and judgment** to navigate the fast-changing world of AI and responsible use of data.
* **Access dedicated career support** from [ESMT’s Career Development Center](https://esmt.berlin/master-programs/careers "Careers"): 1-on-1 coaching, career fairs, and job search preparation throughout your studies.
* **Make Berlin your campus**—an international hub for AI and technology, innovation, and career opportunity.
* **Study in one of Europe’s most active AI and technology ecosystems** and build connections that extend far beyond the classroom.
* **Be part of a diverse, close-knit community** that inspires your ambitions and supports your growth long after graduation.
* **Launch your career in** [**Germany**](https://esmt.berlin/ten-reasons-study-germany) with an extended 18-month residence permit after you graduate.

### Your profile

You are a great fit for the Master in Analytics and Artificial Intelligence if you:

* are a young professional in your twenties with **0-3 years of postgraduate work experience** (applicants with up to five years of experience will be considered on a case-by-case basis)
* have an **intuitive understanding of** how deeply **technology, data, and AI** permeates all aspects of modern life
* quickly grasp and **adapt to new trends in business analytics**
* are **curious to master the complex flows of data**, shaping them to create value for business and society

## What do I need to apply?

### General requirements

* **A previous degree (at least Bachelor's equivalent)** with excellent grades. If you are completing your undergraduate studies within the next year, your application will also be considered.
* Ideally, applicants to the MAAI program will have between **0-3 years of post-graduate work experience**. Applicants with up to five years of post-graduate work experience will be considered for admission on a case-by-case basis, following assessment and alignment of their academic and professional goals to the respective program.
* **English language proficiency** test scores (if applicable)
+ Submitting an English proficiency test score (TOEFL, IELTS or TOEIC) is not mandatory. Your English language skills are also assessed in the selection interview.
+ You will receive a waiver for the English proficiency test if English is your native language or if you have completed a university degree completely taught in English.
+ If required, ESMT expects a minimum score of 100 in the TOEFL, 7.0 in the IELTS, or 850 in the TOEIC. The ESMT TOEFL institution code is 8707.
* **Evidence of strong quantitative skills** - depending on your profile, you may be required to take the BAT or GMAT/GRE test)
+ GMAT or GRE scores are not obligatory if candidates can provide alternative evidence of quantitative skills or scientific training within their Bachelor studies through competitive grades, that included general quantitative courses or with a degree in engineering, natural sciences, mathematics, statistics, or another quantitative field (including economics or business with mainly theoretical/quantitative coursework).
+ As a guideline, GMAT scores below 600 or GRE scores below 304 are not considered competitive. Depending on educational background and the grades, the review of scores can focus more on the quantitative section of the respective test.
+ ESMT Business Admissions Test (BAT) can be offered to MAAI candidates, based on individual assessment (educational background and the grades), or they can receive a waiver for the tests if their academic transcripts or related qualifications demonstrate sufficient quantitative skills.
* Completed application - including essays, one reference, and €75 application fee.

### Interview assessment

During the interview process at ESMT, applicants are assessed on their analytical and problem-solving skills, teamwork abilities, and English language proficiency.

Interviews are conducted in English by trained staff or alumni, using the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR) to ensure candidates meet the B2 level or higher.

### Evidence of scientific training

Evidence of scientific training can be proven from a Bachelor’s degree specialization in one of the following:

* engineering (chemical, civil, electrical, mechanical, environmental, or other engineering application)
* natural sciences (biology, chemistry, physics, or other natural science)
* mathematics and statistics, or
* other quantitative science economics, where the majority of courses and research were theoretical and quantitative in nature rather than applied.
