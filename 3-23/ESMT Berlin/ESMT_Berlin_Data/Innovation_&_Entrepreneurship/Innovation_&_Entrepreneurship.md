# Innovation & Entrepreneurship

## Make your mark as a founder, innovator, investor, or consultant with a master’s in innovation and entrepreneurship

**The Master in Innovation and Entrepreneurship (MIE) is designed for ambitious minds ready to create, disrupt, and bring ideas to life.**

In the program, you will experience the full innovation journey: learning how to identify opportunities, design and test innovative ideas and turn them into real solutions. Whether your goal is to launch a startup or shape innovation within an established organization, the MIE equips you with the mindset, knowledge, and network to make it happen.

Through hands-on projects, mentorship from founders, corporate partners, industry experts, and access to Berlin’s vibrant innovation ecosystem, you will develop the practical skills, business foundations, and trusted network to create meaningful value for business and society.

### Program highlights

* **A hands-on curriculum guiding you through every stage of innovation**—from ideation and validation to scaling and implementation.
* **Two flexible paths** that let you focus on startup entrepreneurship or corporate innovation, with the freedom to switch or combine both as your goals evolve.
* **Built-in opportunities for practical experience** through the Sustainability Bootcamp, Summer Entrepreneurship Program (SEP), Corporate Innovation Project (CIP), DEEP Tech Innovation Program, NEXT Program and Social Impact Project, and 3- to 6-month internships.
* **Build your own MIE journey** with up to 50% customizable program content, electives across all MSc programs, an optional [Finance & Investment Certificate](https://esmt.berlin/degrees/esmt-finance-certificate), and access to Bloomberg, Amazon and SAP’s self-guided certificate courses.
* **Skill-building electives** that can help you strengthen your business network, including clinics in negotiation and communication, and a Corporate Impact Lab where you can connect with top-level corporate leaders, directors, and senior partners.
* **Access to Berlin’s thriving startup ecosystem** through [Vali Berlin - ESMT's Entrepreneurship Hub](https://esmt.berlin/vali) and ESMT’s [Institute for DEEP Tech Innovation](https://esmt.berlin/faculty-research/institutes/deep-institute-deep-tech-innovation).
* **World-class faculty** who actively shape innovation and entrepreneurship through research, executive education, and as advisors in global companies.

### Why choose master’s in innovation and entrepreneurship at ESMT Berlin

* **Gain confidence and resilience** to bring ideas to life and drive meaningful change.
* **Understand how business works** so you can build, challenge, or transform it.
* **Pursue ideas that matter to you**, supported by mentors, peers, and an ecosystem that values experimentation and impact.
* **Access tailored guidance and coaching** from [Vali Berlin,](https://esmt.berlin/vali) [Institute for DEEP Tech Innovatio](https://esmt.berlin/faculty-research/institutes/deep-institute-deep-tech-innovation)n and [ESMT’s Career Development Center](https://esmt.berlin/master-programs/careers), connecting you with companies, founders, and industry experts.
* **Grow in Berlin, a city that never stands still**, offering the perfect environment to experiment, collaborate, and launch your ideas.
* **Be part of a diverse, close-knit community** that opens doors, inspires your ambitions, and supports your growth long after graduation.
* **Launch your career in** [**Germany**](https://esmt.berlin/ten-reasons-study-germany) with an extended 18-month residence permit after you graduate.

### Your profile

You are a great fit for the Master in Innovation and Entrepreneurship if you:

* are a young professional in your twenties with **0-3 years of postgraduate work experience** (applicants with up to five years of postgraduate work experience will be considered on a case-by-case basis)
* are eager to get **hands-on experience**, build your network and prepare for a career in innovation. You might even have your own startup idea that you want to develop.
* are interested in joining the world of innovation and entrepreneurship either as a **startup founder, venture capitalist, innovation consultant or corporate innovator**
* are a technologist who wants to **learn how to turn new tools and tech into viable businesses** and develop the skills to assess, analyze, and take calculated risks
* are a creative mind who **thinks in opportunities** and see ideas everywhere

## What do I need to apply?

### General requirements

* **A previous degree** (at least Bachelor's equivalent) with excellent grades. If you are finishing your undergraduate studies within the next year, your application will also be considered.
* Ideally, applicants to the MIE program will have between **0-3 years of post-graduate work experience**. Applicants with up to five years of post-graduate work experience will be considered for admission on a case-by-case basis, following assessment and alignment of their academic and professional goals to the respective program.
* **English language proficiency** test scores (if applicable)
+ Submitting an English proficiency test score (TOEFL, IELTS or TOEIC) is not mandatory. Your English language skills are also assessed in the selection interview.
+ You will receive a waiver for the English proficiency test if English is your native language or if you have completed a university degree completely taught in English.
+ If required, ESMT expects a minimum score of 100 in the TOEFL, 7.0 in the IELTS, or 850 in the TOEIC. The ESMT TOEFL institution code is 8707.
* **Entrance exam (if applicable)** - depending on your profile, you may be required to take the BAT or GMAT/GRE test
+ GMAT or GRE scores are not obligatory if candidates can provide alternative evidence of quantitative skills or scientific training within their Bachelor studies through competitive grades, that included general quantitative courses or with a degree in engineering, natural sciences, mathematics, statistics, or another quantitative field (including economics or business with mainly theoretical/quantitative coursework).
+ ESMT does not have a set minimum GMAT/GRE scores. As a guideline, GMAT scores below 600 (GMAT Focus below 555), or GRE scores below 304 are usually not considered competitive. Depending on educational background and the grades, the review of scores can focus more on the quantitative section of the respective test.
+ Candidates may also take the ESMT Business Admissions Test (BAT) or receive a waiver for the tests if their academic transcripts or related qualifications demonstrate sufficient quantitative skills.
* **Completed application** - including essays, one reference, and €75 application fee.

### Interview assessment

During the interview process at ESMT, applicants are assessed on their analytical and problem-solving skills, teamwork abilities, and English language proficiency.

Interviews are conducted in English by trained staff or alumni, using the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR) to ensure candidates meet the B2 level or higher.
