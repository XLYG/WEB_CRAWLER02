# Global Management

## Explore business and discover your passion with a master's in global management

**The Master in Global Management (MGM) is designed for those who want to build an international career that spans industries and borders.**

Whether you have a clear direction or are still exploring your interests, the program equips you with general management expertise and the skills to think across functions, tackle challenges from multiple perspectives, and apply solutions in global business contexts.

**ESMT Berlin is currently ranked #1 in Germany in the** [**Financial Times Global Masters in Management Ranking 2025**](https://rankings.ft.com/rankings/3004/masters-in-management-2025 "(opens in a new window)") **and #7 globally in international work mobility.** We achieved leading scores in Career Progress, Number of international students, and ESG and Net Zero teaching among all represented German schools.

### Program highlights

* **A globally focused curriculum** covering all relevant general management disciplines from strategy to finance, taught through interactive, and practice-oriented teaching methodologies.
* **Customizable learning paths** with 50% elective flexibility, cross-program course options, and optional certificates – including a [Finance & Investment Certificate](https://esmt.berlin/degrees/esmt-finance-certificate "ESMT Finance and Investment Certificate"), and access to Bloomberg’s Certificate Courses.
* **Built-in opportunities for hands-on experience**, including 3 to 6-month internships, social impact projects, and applied consulting projects.
* **Skill-building electives** that can enrich and broaden your business knowledge and network, including clinics in negotiation and communication, and a Corporate Impact Lab taught by top-level corporate leaders, directors, and senior partners.
* **International exposure** through study abroad at [20+ partner schools](https://esmt.berlin/master-programs/master-global-management/international-options), the [Global Impact Program](https://esmt.berlin/master-programs/master-global-management/global-impact-program/) with University of Virginia, and a double degree option with [Yale SOM.](https://esmt.berlin/degrees/double-degree-yale/)
* **Top-ranked** [**faculty**](https://esmt.berlin/master-programs/master-program-faculty) trained at leading institutions worldwide, who teach executive education and advise global companies, bringing real-world experience into the classroom.

### Why choose the Master in Global Management at ESMT Berlin

* **Gain experience that matters** through practical projects and real business challenges.
* **Access dedicated career support** from [ESMT’s Career Development Center](https://esmt.berlin/master-programs/careers): 1-on-1 coaching, career fairs, and job search preparation throughout your studies.
* **Make Berlin your campus**—an international hub for ideas, innovation, and career opportunity.
* **Be part of a diverse, close-knit community** with small class sizes, international peers, and a strong global alumni network.
* **Launch your** [**career in Germany**](https://esmt.berlin/knowledge/study-in-germany-guide) with an extended 18-month residence permit after you graduate.

### Your profile

You are a great fit for the Master in Global Management if you:

* are a young professional in your early twenties with **no more than two years of postgraduate work experience** (excluding internships and work during your bachelor studies)
* are curious about **global business** and open to **cultural diversity**
* want to develop **management and leadership skills** for an interconnected world
* see **sustainable business** as key to creating a positive future
* value teamwork and are ready to grow into responsible, **impact-driven** leaders
* are looking for an experience **outside your comfort zone** to define the impact you want to have professionally and personally

## Admission requirements

The admissions process for the [Master in Global Management](https://esmt.berlin/master-programs/master-global-management) is designed to identify candidates who will thrive in the program’s collaborative, international, and interdisciplinary environment.

**When evaluating applications, the Admissions Committee looks for the following qualities:**

* A desire to drive positive change through business
* An international outlook, intercultural competency, and a responsible approach to business
* Potential for high-performance career growth in a global environment
* Willingness to learn from others and demonstrate collegiality under pressure
* A strategic mindset, intellectual curiosity, and analytical rigor
* Willingness to actively engage in and contribute to the ESMT network, enriching peer learning inside and outside the classroom
* Potential to serve as an ambassador for the school’s mission globally

## What do I need to apply?

### General requirements

* **A previous degree (at least Bachelor's equivalent)** with excellent grades. If you are completing your undergraduate studies within the next year, your application will also be considered.
* Recently graduated, with **no more than 24 months of postgraduate work experience** (excluding internships)
* **English language proficiency** test scores (if applicable)
+ Submitting an English proficiency test score (TOEFL, IELTS or TOEIC) is not mandatory. Your English language skills are also assessed in the selection interview.
+ You will receive a waiver for the English proficiency test if English is your native language or if you have completed a university degree completely taught in English.
+ If required, ESMT expects a minimum score of 100 in the TOEFL, 7.0 in the IELTS, or 850 in the TOEIC. The ESMT TOEFL institution code is 8707.
* **Evidence of quantitative skills** - depending on your profile, you may be required to take the BAT or GMAT/GRE test)
+ GMAT or GRE scores are not obligatory if candidates can provide alternative evidence of quantitative skills or scientific training within their Bachelor studies through competitive grades, that included general quantitative courses or with a degree in engineering, natural sciences, mathematics, statistics, or another quantitative field (including economics or business with mainly theoretical/quantitative coursework).
+ As a guideline, GMAT scores below 600 or GRE scores below 304 are not considered competitive. Depending on educational background and the grades, the review of scores can focus more on the quantitative section of the respective test.
+ Candidates may also take the ESMT Business Admissions Test (BAT) or receive a waiver for the tests if their academic transcripts or related qualifications demonstrate sufficient quantitative skills.
* **Completed application** - including essays, one reference, and €75 application fee.

### Interview assessment

During the interview process at ESMT, applicants are assessed on their **analytical and problem-solving skills**, **teamwork abilities**, and **English language proficiency**.

Interviews are conducted in English by trained staff or alumni, using the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR) to ensure candidates meet the B2 level or higher.
