## WHY PARTICIPATE IN A DOUBLE DEGREE PROGRAM?

## Receive two master's degrees from Austria and Germany in one program 

Students who successfully complete the double degree program will receive an Austrian master's degree from FH Kärnten/Carinthia University of Applied Sciences (CUAS) as well as a German master's degree from TH Köln/Cologne University of Applied Sciences (THK) after four semesters. CUAS students who are participating in the double degree program spend their second semester abroad in the beautiful city of Cologne. THK students spend their third semester in the fascinating scenery of Carinthia.

## Gain international experience, build your network and develop yourself 

Participants in the double degree program get the unique chance to study at two renowned higher education institutions in Austria and Germany and gain international experience through studying abroad.Learning about European economy and culture from a Western as well as from a Central and South Eastern European perspective will broaden the students'horizon. They can also build an international network and use their time abroad for personal development.

## Enjoy a unique combination of metropolitan life and idyllic lakes and mountains 

CUAS is located in the South of Austria, embedded between beautiful lakes and stunning mountains.Depending on the season, students can ride down the skiing slopes or jump into the refreshing lakes before class. THK is Germany's largest university of applied sciences. The city of Cologne with its over 1 million inhabitants is a cultural center with lively quarters and over 100,o00 students.

For International Business Management (CUAS) and International Business (THK) Master students 

## HOW TO APPLY FOR THE DOUBLE DEGREE PROGRAM?

In order to be eligible for taking part in the Carinthia-Cologne double degree program, you need to be enrolled in the International Business Management master's program at CUAS or in the International Business master's program at THK 



The general entry requirements for both master degrees must be met 

Complete the application form for the double degree program and include the required appendices (see the application form)



Required level of English is B2

.Include GMAT test results (5oo points or more) or equivalent GRE test results 

## WHAT ARE THE REQUIREMENTS FOR THE SUCCESSFUL COMPLETION OF THE DOUBLE DEGREE PROGRAM? 

•Successful application for the double degree program 

Completion of all parts of the 4-semester double degree curriculum including one semester abroad at the partner institution (2nd semester in Cologne for CUAS students, 3rd semester in Carinthia for THK students)

A positively approved master thesis (with the first supervisor at the home institution and the second assessor at the host institution)

Successfully passed master exams at both CUAS and THK
