## § 1 Scope, Study Objectives

(1) The subject-specific provisions regulate the content and requirements of the Master's degree programme in Medical Physics at Faculty 04 LSE.

(2) Graduates of the Master's degree programme in Medical Physics are able to solve tasks and problems in the interdisciplinary field of Medical Physics using scientific methods, analyse and critically classify new scientific findings and use them in professional practice on the basis of their mathematical and scientific expertise and their basic medical knowledge. They are capable of creative, independent and autonomous scientific work.

The social and personal skills they have acquired enable them to play an active role in an interdisciplinary team, mainly consisting of physicians, physicists and engineers, and to represent their point of view in a scientifically sound manner in presentations and discussions. As future medical physics experts, they are aware of their special responsibility towards patients.

(3) The Master's degree programme in Medical Physics can be studied in the German-language or English-language version, provided that the admission and admission requirements in accordance with §§ 2 and 3 have been met. It is only possible to change the study programme version if the admission and admission requirements for the other study programme variant are met. If at least 66 CrP of the 90 CrP of the degree programme were completed in English, the final documents will indicate that the degree programme was studied in the English degree programme version.
## § 2 Acceptance Requirements

(1) The Master's degree programme in Medical Physics builds consecutively on a first professionally qualifying university degree with a standard period of study of 7 semesters (210 credit points) in the Medical Physics degree programme at the Technische Hochschule Mittelhessen. The degree programme can also build on a first professionally qualifying university degree with a standard period of study of 7 semesters (210 CP) in a related degree programme. In this case, the first professionally qualifying university degree must contain scientific, engineering and subject-specific components to approximately the same extent as the Bachelor's degree programme in Medical Physics and Radiation Protection at the Technische Hochschule Mittelhessen. The examination board decides on the fulfilment of this condition in consultation with the head of the degree programme.

(2) Admission to the Master's degree programme in Biomedical Engineering in the German-language version is only possible if proof of a first professionally qualifying university degree according to para. 1 can be provided and additionally either the overall grade of this first professionally qualifying university degree is at least 'satisfactory' (2.7; at least 70 percentage points according to § 9 para. 2 of Part I of the examination regulations) or proof of passing an aptitude test of the LSE department for the Master's programme in Biomedical Engineering from the summer semester 2025 or earlier.

Admission to the Master's degree programme in Biomedical Engineering in the English-language version is only possible if proof of a first professionally qualifying university degree in accordance with Paragraph 1 can be provided and additionally either the overall grade of this first professionally qualifying university degree is at least 'satisfactory' (2.7; at least 70 percentage points in accordance with Section 9 Paragraph 2 of Part I of the Examination Regulations) or proof of passing an aptitude test of the LSE department for the Master's programme in Biomedical Engineering from the summer semester 2025 or earlier.

(3) If the standard period of study for the first professionally qualifying university degree is less than 7 semesters, Section 4 (5) must also be observed.

(4) Foreign applicants for the Master's degree programme in Medical Physics who have obtained their first professionally qualifying university degree in a non-German-language degree programme must provide proof of German language proficiency in accordance with the 'Framework Regulations on German Language Examinations for Studies at German Universities', as amended, at the latest by the end of the enrolment period for the Master's degree programme in Medical Physics set by the THM. Corresponding certificates include the German Language Test for University Admission (DSH-2), or the Test of German as a Foreign Language (TestDaF with at least 4 points in each section). If these applicants wish to pursue the English-language version of the degree programme, they must provide proof of German language skills at level A1 of the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages by the end of the enrolment period for the Master's in Medical Physics set by the THM.

## § 3 Entrance qualifications

(1) Admission to the Master's degree programme in Medical Physics requires the fully completed 'Application for admission to the Master's degree programme in Medical Physics as well as the decision for the English or German-language study programme variant according to § 1 para. 3 via the portal specified by THM within the application deadline specified by THM, including the following documents:

1. Proof of the first professionally qualifying university degree according to § 2 para. 1 to 3 by means of

a) a degree certificate.

b) a Diploma Supplement and

c) a Transcript of Records or comparable proof

If these final documents of the first professionally qualifying university degree cannot be submitted by the end of the application deadline, § 4 para. 7 applies.

2. Curriculum vitae in tabular form
3. For the German-language study programme version: Proof of sufficient English language skills at level B1 of the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages. For the English-language study programme variant: Proof of sufficient English language skills at level B2 of the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages.

The required language skills are usually demonstrated by the TOEFL test, TOEIC test or IELTS test. The examination board decides on the recognition of the proof in consultation with the head of the degree programme.

4. Foreign applicants who have obtained their first professionally qualifying university degree in a non-German-language degree programme: Proof of German language skills in accordance with § 2 Para. 4. If a language certificate cannot be submitted by the end of the application deadline, § 4 Para. 6 applies.

(2) All documents to be submitted in accordance with para. 1 must be issued in German or English. If documents are issued in a language other than German or English, an officially recognised translation of the documents into German or English must be obtained and submitted with the original documents.

## § 4 Admission decision

(1) The university decides on the recognition of foreign or equivalent degrees and university entrance qualifications in accordance with the statutory provisions and the requirements of the Conference of University Rectors and the Conference of Ministers of Education and Cultural Affairs.

(2) Admission to the Master's degree programme in Medicsl Physics is granted to those who fulfil the admission requirements according to § 2 and the admission requirements according to § 3. The Examination Board decides on the fulfilment of all subject-specific admission requirements in consultation with the head of degree programme. It may consult other professors or academic staff for a preliminary examination of the application documents. In cases of doubt, it can request further documents from the applicant, such as certificates of previous relevant professional activities, as well as further education and training courses.

(3) Admission is granted by the President (Registrar's Office) of the Technische Hochschule Mittelhessen. The applicant must be notified in writing of any rejection decisions. The decision must include information on legal remedies.

(4) Applicants who lack the knowledge and skills from their first professionally qualifying university degree that are required in modules of the Master's degree programme in MedicsI Physics may be admitted to the Master's degree programme in Biomedical Engineering subject to certain conditions. They must acquire the missing knowledge and skills from the Bachelor's degree programmes at THM, in particular the Bachelor's degree programme in MedicsI Physics. The admission decision and the determination of the knowledge and skills to be made up for is made by the examination board in consultation with the head of the degree programme. No knowledge and competences may be missing than those that can be made up within the scope of 30 CrP. The missing knowledge and skills must be demonstrated by completing modules (which are offered in the language of the chosen degree programme version) from the Bachelor's degree programme in Biomedical Engineering and/or an assessment test, which is carried out in the department upon application, by the time of admission to the Master's thesis at the latest. Proof is provided when the assessment test has been passed. In the event of failure, the assessment test can be repeated. Students will be informed in good time and in an appropriate manner about the manner in which the assessment test will be conducted. The knowledge and competences proven by an assessment test are not part of the Master's degree programme and are not credited. The modules completed as compensation are certified in the transcript of records. However, they are also not part of the Master's degree programme. CrP earned are not credited in the Master's programme, and grades earned are not taken into account when calculating the final grade.

(5) Bachelor's graduates from degree programmes with a standard period of study of six semesters (180 CP) can be admitted to the Master's degree programme in Medicsl Physics subject to certain conditions. During the Master's degree programme, they must complete additional modules amounting to 30 CP from the THM's range of modules and provide evidence of this by the time of admission to the Master's thesis, so that a total volume of 300 credit points can be demonstrated.
after completion of the Master's degree programme in Biomedical Engineering. The modules to be completed are determined individually by the examination board in consultation with the head of the degree programme based on the course content completed as part of the previous degree. The additionally completed modules are certified in the transcript of records. However, they are not part of the Master's degree programme. CrP earned are not credited in the Master's programme, and grades earned are not taken into account when calculating the final grade.

(6) Applicants who do not fulfil all the admission requirements specified in § 3 by the end of the respective application deadline may be admitted to the Master's degree programme subject to the proviso that proof of the missing requirements will be provided no later than the end of the enrolment period for the Master's degree programme in Medical Physics specified by THM. In case of non-completion, the conditional admission will expire.

(7) Applicants who have not yet completed their first professionally qualifying university degree programme by the application deadline must submit a detailed certificate on the status of this degree programme instead of the degree documents required in Section 3 (1) No. 1 in order to be granted conditional admission to the Master's degree programme.

This certificate must show the following:

- all academic results to date from the first professionally qualifying degree programme,

- the current number of credit points earned and the total number of credit points to be earned; of the total number of credit points to be earned, at least 70% must have already been earned,

• the current average grade.

By the end of the enrolment period for the Master's programme in Biomedical Engineering specified by THM, the first professionally qualifying university degree must have been completed with an overall grade of at least 'satisfactory' (at least 70 percentage points according to § 9 (2) of Part I of the examination regulations 2.7) or the first professionally qualifying university degree and the passed aptitude test according to § 2 (2). Failure to do so will result in the conditional admission being revoked.

## § 5 Matriculation

Admitted applicants must enrolled by the deadline set by THM. You can only be enrolled if you fulfil all enrolment requirements in accordance with Section 3 of the THM Enrolment Statutes as amended.

## § 6 Master's Degrees and Certificates

Upon successful completion of the Master's degree programme in Medical Physics, the academic degree 'Master of Science', abbreviated to 'M. Sc.", is awarded with a certificate in accordance with Appendix 4.

## § 7 Standard period of study, duration and structure of the programme

The standard period of study for the Master's degree programme in Medical Physics is 3 semesters, which corresponds to 1.5 academic years. To successfully complete the Master's examination, the modules listed in the module overview in Appendix 1 must be successfully completed. Admission to the programme is possible in both the summer and winter semesters (see Appendix 1).

## § 8 Module, Language, Master's thesis, Colloquium

(1) The modules to be completed must be taken from the modules offered in the Master's degree programme in Medical Physics in accordance with Appendix 1 and 2. Alternatively, identical or equivalent modules can also be taken from the modules offered in other Master's degree programmes at the Technische Hochschule Mittelhessen or other universities. Any failed attempts will be recorded. Sections 11 to 14 of the General Provisions (Part I of the Examination Regulations) apply.

(2) In the case of an examination in which the demonstration of successful completion is a written examination, the last repetition can be carried out as an oral examination in accordance with Section 7 of the General Provisions at the written request of the candidate. This does not apply to the project phase, project work and the Master's thesis.
(3) Participation in internships requires registration by the deadline published by the Dean's Office. The registration deadline may be at the end of the course period of the previous semester.

(4) For compulsory elective modules, binding registration is required before the end of the lecture period of the previous semester. If there are fewer than 6 binding registrations, there is no entitlement to attend these courses.

(5) In an ‘accelerated procedure’, compulsory elective modules that have not yet been offered which address current topics and are of interest to the students, can be offered by the faculty without a change in the examination regulations being made in advance. The procedural requirements for this are regulated in Appendix 2 (module handbook).

(6) The language of instruction and examination is generally German. Other examination languages may be specified in the module handbook (Appendix 2).

(7) The prerequisite for starting the Master's thesis is the completion of modules in the first year of the Master's programme amounting to at least 45 credit points (CrP).

(8) The processing time for the Master's thesis is 25 weeks and has a value of 30 credit points. Two written copies of the Master's thesis must be submitted to the departmental office by the deadline. In addition, the Master's thesis must be submitted in digital form.

(9) The student must present and defend his or her Master's thesis in a colloquium for the Master's thesis. The assessment of the colloquium is included proportionately in the grade for the Master's thesis. Further details are set out in the module handbook (Appendix 2). The colloquium for the Master's thesis is the last part of the examination to be completed. As a rule, the colloquium takes place in public at the university, provided that the content of the Master's thesis is not subject to a confidentiality obligation.

## § 9 Obligation to pay costs

No fees are charged for the Master's degree programme in Medical Physics in accordance with Section 20 (5) HessHG. The obligation to pay the semester fee according to § 83 para. 3 HessHG, the administrative fee according to § 62 HessHG as well as fees and contributions according to other legal regulations remains unaffected.

## § 10 Effective date, transitional provisions

(1) These examination regulations come into force on 01 October 2016. They apply to all students commencing their studies on the Master's degree programme in Biomedical Engineering from the 2016/2017 winter semester onwards.

(2) For students who commenced their studies on the Master's degree course in Medical Physics before these examination regulations came into force, the examination regulations of Faculty 04 LSE dated 9 January 2008 (StAnz 19/2008 p. 1058), last amended on 17 April 2013 (AMB 40/2013), shall apply at the latest until the end of the winter semester 2017/18.

(3) According to para. 2, students can irrevocably declare at any time that the conditions of the examination regulations valid from 1 October 2016 should apply to them. From 1 April 2018, these examination regulations will be binding for all students on the Master's degree programme in Medical Physics. The previously valid examination regulations dated 09 January 2008 (StAnz 19/2008 p. 1058), last amended on 17 April 2013 (AMB 40/2013), will be discontinued as of 01 April 2018.

Giessen, 20 July 2016
