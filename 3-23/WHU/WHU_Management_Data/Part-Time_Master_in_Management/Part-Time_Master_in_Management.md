## Detailed Admissions Requirements (Appendix)

Source URL: https://www.whu.edu/en/programs/master-of-arts-programs/part-time-master-in-management/application-admissions/

### Our admission requirements

[Bachelor’s degree or equivalent](#accordion-13682-item-1)

You have successfully completed an accredited or similarly recognized B.Sc or B.A. degree of at least 240 ECTS credits. Candidates with fewer than 240 ECTS credits may be admitted on an individual basis if they meet the other admissions requirements.

[Minimum of one year professional working experience](#accordion-13682-item-2)

We recommend that you have at least one year's practical experience when you start your studies. This can include working as a student trainee, internships, vocational training,a dual study program, project activities, etc.

[International experience of at least three months](#accordion-13682-item-3)

As international experience we accept e.g. internships abroad, language courses, semester abroad, summer schools, or high school exchange year in non-German speaking country. This requirement must only be fulfilled by candidates from Germany, Austria, and Switzerland.

[Language skills in English certified by standardized test or equivalent verification (e.g. nationality, semester abroad, business language)](#accordion-13682-item-4)

* TOEFL iBT: minimum score of 100 (classic scoring system, code: 0682) or minimum 5.5 points (new format, valid from January 2026) and a minimum score of 23 in speaking and writing. WHU code: 0682
* OR IELTS with an overall score of at least 7.0 and a minimum score of 6.0 in speaking and writing
* OR Cambridge Certificate of Proficiency in English (CPE) of at least Grade C
* OR Cambridge Certificate of Advance in English (CAE)of Grade A

PLEASE NOTE: We also accept the **TOEFL iBT Special Home Edition** that you can take online.

An English language proficiency test is not required if your first language is English or your Bachelor was completed entirely in English. In this case, proof from your university is required.

[Proof of strong analytical and quantitative skills (GMAT/GRE/TM-BASE)](#accordion-13682-item-5)

* **GMAT**: Minimum score 555 - WHU Code: FBR-FH-93 (GMAT 10th Edition: Minimum 600)
* **GRE**: Minimum score 158 Verbal and 158 Quantitative - WHU Code: 7175
* **GTEBS** (formerly TM-WISO): Minimum score 102 (must be taken in English)

You can also take any of these tests from home - we accept their online versions.
