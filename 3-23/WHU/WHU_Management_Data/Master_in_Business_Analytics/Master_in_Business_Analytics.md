### Admission Requirements

* ##### **Academic background: Bachelor’s degree in business, STEM, or a quantitatively oriented field**

If the degree was obtained outside Germany, the university must be listed in the [**Anabin**](https://anabin.kmk.org/db/institutionen) database as “H+” to confirm recognition.
* ##### **Practical experience: Minimum 3 months, maximum 6 years**

The required 3 months of full-time, business-related experience may be completed by August 15. Please submit an official employer certificate that includes your name, position, employment dates, and responsibilities, along with the company stamp and signature.
* ##### **Outstanding Quantitative Skills: Top quantitative grades or test scores**

Applicants must demonstrate strong quantitative ability through **one**of the following:

+ **Excellent grades in quantitative** courses during the bachelor’s degree
+ **GMAT**: min. 555 (code: FBR-FH-93)
+ **GRE**: min. 158/158 (code: 7175)
+ **GTEBS** min. 102 (in English)

You may apply without the score and submit it by May 31. Online test versions are accepted. You may find the following pages helpful when preparing for the tests: [GMAT prep](https://www.mba.com/exams/gmat-exam/prep-for-the-exam "Go to GMAC Prep for the Exam website"), [GMAT quiz](https://www.mba.com/gmat-mini-quiz "Go to the GMAT Mini Quiz website"), [GRE prep](https://www.ets.org/gre/test-takers/general-test/prepare.html?WT.ac=grehome_greprepare_b_150213 "Go to Official GRE Prep website"), [GRE quiz](https://www.ets.org/gre/gre-mini-quiz.html "Go to GRE Mini Quiz website"), [GTEBS info](https://itb-academic-tests.org/en/universities/gtebs/ "Go to ITB Test website") (formerly TM-WISO).
* ##### **English proficiency: IELTS or TOEFL**

One of the following tests (valid for two years; online versions accepted):

+ **IELTS Academic**: min. 7.0
+ **TOEFL iBT**: min. 100 (code: 0682) or 5.5 (new format)

The requirement is waived if your bachelor’s degree or secondary education was entirely taught in English (official proof required). You may apply without a test score and submit it by May 31.
* ##### **International experience:**

Applicants from Germany, Austria, and Switzerland applying for the 90-credit track must provide proof of at least three months of international experience (e.g., internship, exchange semester, summer school, language course) before starting the program.
