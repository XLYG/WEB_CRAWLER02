### Admission requirements

##### **Academic background: Bachelor’s degree in business or economics**

Applicants must hold a bachelor’s from an accredited institution with a minimum of 180 ECTS credits, either in a business-related field of study, or from STEM subjects with a strong quantitative background and practical experience in finance-related areas. If the degree was obtained outside Germany, the university must be listed as “H+” in the [Anabin](https://anabin.kmk.org/db/institutionen) database to confirm its equivalence to a German higher education institution.

##### **Practical experience: Minimum 3 months, maximum 4 years**

The program is designed for candidates with no more than four years of postgraduate work experience. Applicants must have at least 12 weeks (480 hours) of practical experience in a business-related role. This requirement can be fulfilled before the start of your studies at WHU. Please submit a certificate or official letter from your employer that includes your name, position title, start and end dates, a brief description of your responsibilities, as well as the company stamp and an official signature.

##### **Quantitative & analytical proficiency: GMAT, GRE or GTEBS**

We require one of the following test scores (online formats are accepted):

* **GMAT**: min. 555 (Code: FBR-FH-93)
* **GRE**: min. 158/158 - Verbal and Quantitative (Code: 7175)
* **GTEBS** (formerly TM-WISO): min. 102 (must be taken in English)

Helpful resources: [GMAT prep](https://www.mba.com/exams/gmat-exam/prep-for-the-exam "Go to GMAC Prep for the Exam website"), [GMAT quiz](https://www.mba.com/gmat-mini-quiz "Go to the GMAT Mini Quiz website"), [GRE prep](https://www.ets.org/gre/test-takers/general-test/prepare.html?WT.ac=grehome_greprepare_b_150213 "Go to Official GRE Prep website"), [GRE quiz](https://www.ets.org/gre/gre-mini-quiz.html "Go to GRE Mini Quiz website"), [GTEBS info](https://itb-academic-tests.org/en/universities/gtebs/ "Go to ITB Test website")

**You can apply now without a test score and submit it later. If admitted, your offer will be conditional until the score is received.**

##### **English proficiency: IELTS or TOEFL**

We require one of the following test scores (valid for two years; online formats are accepted):

* **IELTS Academic**: min. 7.0
* **TOEFL iBT:** min. 100 (classic scoring system, code: 0682) or min. 5.5 (new format, valid from January 2026)

This requirement is waived if your entire bachelor's degree or secondary education was taught in English (proof required). We do **not**accept alternative tests such as Duolingo English Test or TOEIC.

**You can apply now without a test score and submit it later. If admitted, your offer will be conditional until the score is received.**

##### **International experience: Recommended**

Candidates from Germany, Austria, and Switzerland applying for the 90-credit track are required to provide proof of international experience of approximately three months before the start of their studies at WHU. Accepted forms of experience include internships or exchange semester abroad, language courses, summer schools, or comparable international activities. For all other candidates, such experience is not mandatory but will enhance your application, as internationality is one of our core values.

**APS certificate (India, China, Vietnam)**
Applicants with a bachelor’s degree from **India**, **China**, or **Vietnam** must obtain an **APS certificate** before applying for a German student visa. We recommend starting the APS process early, as it may take several months.
