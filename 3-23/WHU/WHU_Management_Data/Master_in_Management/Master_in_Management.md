### Admission requirements

##### **Academic background: Bachelor’s degree in business or economics.**

Applicants must hold a bachelor’s degree in business or economics from an accredited institution with a minimum of 180 ECTS credits. If the degree was obtained outside Germany, the university must be listed as “H+” in the [Anabin](https://anabin.kmk.org/db/institutionen) database to confirm its equivalence to a German higher education institution.

##### **Practical experience: Minimum 3 months, maximum 2 years.**

The program is designed for candidates with no more than two years of postgraduate work experience. Applicants must have at least 12 weeks (480 hours) of practical experience in a business-related role. This requirement can be fulfilled before the start of your studies at WHU. Please submit a certificate or official letter from your employer that includes your name, position title, start and end dates, a brief description of your responsibilities, as well as the company stamp and an official signature.

##### **Quantitative & analytical proficiency: GMAT, GRE or GTEBS.**

We require one of the following test scores (online formats are accepted):

* **GMAT**: min. 555 (Code: FBR-FH-93)
* **GRE**: min. 158/158 - Verbal and Quantitative (Code: 7175)
* **GTEBS** (formerly TM-WISO): min. 102 (must be taken in English)

Helpful resources: [GMAT prep](https://www.mba.com/exams/gmat-exam/prep-for-the-exam "Go to GMAC Prep for the Exam website"), [GMAT quiz](https://www.mba.com/gmat-mini-quiz "Go to the GMAT Mini Quiz website"), [GRE prep](https://www.ets.org/gre/test-takers/general-test/prepare.html?WT.ac=grehome_greprepare_b_150213 "Go to Official GRE Prep website"), [GRE quiz](https://www.ets.org/gre/gre-mini-quiz.html "Go to GRE Mini Quiz website"), [GTEBS info](https://itb-academic-tests.org/en/universities/gtebs/ "Go to ITB Test website")

**You can apply now without a test score and submit it later. If admitted, your offer will be conditional until the score is received.**

##### **English proficiency: IELTS or TOEFL.**

We require one of the following test scores (valid for two years; online formats are accepted):

* **IELTS Academic**: min. 7.0
* **TOEFL iBT:** min. 100 (classic scoring system, code: 0682) or min. 5.5 (new format, valid from January 2026)

Not required if your undergraduate or secondary education was completed entirely in English. In that case, please submit proof (e.g., IB diploma, certificate of medium of instruction). We do **not**accept alternative tests such as Duolingo English Test or TOEIC.

**You can apply now without a test score and submit it later. If admitted, your offer will be conditional until the score is received.**

##### **International experience: Recommended.**

International experience is not a formal requirement for this program, but it is highly recommended. As internationality is a core value at WHU, such experience will strengthen your application. This may include a study semester abroad, internship abroad, language course, summer school, or a similar opportunity.

### Application deadlines

* **April 30, 2026** – For applicants requiring a student visa
* **May 31, 2026** – For EU/EEA citizens and applicants who do not require a student visa to study in Germany (e.g., nationals of Australia, Brazil, Canada, Israel, Japan, New Zealand, South Korea, the UK, or the USA)

**APS certificate (India, China, Vietnam)**
Applicants with a bachelor’s degree from **India**, **China**, or **Vietnam** must obtain an **APS certificate** before applying for a German student visa. We recommend starting the APS process early, as it may take several months. More info: [APS India](https://aps-india.de/) | [APS China](https://www.aps.org.cn/zh/) | [APS Vietnam](https://vietnam.diplo.de/vn-vi/%C4%91%E1%BB%A9c-vi%E1%BB%87tnam/2546866-2546866)
