## Introduction

**The Master’s programme ‘Experimental Geosciences’ pursues training and research in one of the central areas in modern Earth Sciences: the experimental simulation of processes occurring in the Earth. This includes the characterization of physical and chemical properties of Earth and planetary materials to advance our understanding of such properties. The M.Sc. programme ‘Experimental Geosciences’ encompasses a number of traditional scientific fields: mineralogy, crystallography, solid state physics, inorganic chemistry, material sciences, geochemistry and cosmochemistry, as well as geophysics. Only by integrating these diverse subfields can one fully understand the structure and dynamics of the Earth and other planets.**

## Profile of the degree programme

The M.Sc. programme ‘Experimental Geosciences’ is centred on the expertise of [Bayerisches Geoinstitut](https://www.bgi.uni-bayreuth.de/?&lng=en) (Bavarian Research Institute of Experimental Geochemistry and Geophysics, BGI). BGI is a central research facility at the University of Bayreuth and a global leader in research and training in experimental geochemistry and geophysics. The institute is also involved in materials science research.

In the M.Sc. programme, students are integrated into small research groups right from the beginning. They work on independent projects under the close and personal supervision of experienced scientists. The laboratory-based work is supplemented by lectures as well as literature and research seminars. The language of instruction is English.

## Career prospects

Over the course of their studies, students acquire strong technical, analytical, and laboratory skills and fundamental scientific knowledge. Master students will graduate with a solid expertise in experimental geosciences focused on understanding earth and planetary materials. This training will allow them to pursue a career in academia (research and technical positions), or in industry (such as geotechnical and mining companies, commercial laboratories), government agencies and national laboratories.

### Admission requirements

Qualification
:   A degree with an examination grade of “good” (2.5) or better in a bachelor’s programme in the natural sciences at the University of Bayreuth or an equivalent degree awarded in Germany or abroad

Equivalency
:   The equivalency of of the completed bachelor's degree is determined conclusively by the board of examiners after the application deadline; if in doubt, clarify details of the application with the programme coordinator prior to submitting it; content-related requirements are specified in §2.2 of the [Examination Regulations](https://www.amtliche-bekanntmachungen.uni-bayreuth.de/de/pruefungsordnungen/master/index.html#Experimental%20Geosciences)

Aptitude Assessment Process
:   One prerequisite for admission to the programme is passing an aptitude assessment process (see [Examination Regulations](https://www.amtliche-bekanntmachungen.uni-bayreuth.de/de/pruefungsordnungen/master/index.html#Experimental%20Geosciences), Annex 2); it includes an evaluation of the applicant's qualifications and, if necessary, a personal interview

Language proficiency
:   [ENGLISH](https://www.international-office.uni-bayreuth.de/en/come-to-bayreuth/degree-students/evidence-of-language-proficiency/index.html): level B2

Applying without a degree certificate
:   Applications may already be submitted before completion of the bachelor's degree; however, a total of at least 150 credits and a minimum grade of "good" (2.5) are required by the time of application

Special conditions
:   Conditional admission is possible; special conditions may comprise up to 12 credit points (see [Examination Regulations](https://www.amtliche-bekanntmachungen.uni-bayreuth.de/de/pruefungsordnungen/master/index.html#Experimental%20Geosciences) §2.2)
