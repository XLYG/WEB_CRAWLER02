## Introduction

The complex world of food is of great importance for many areas of society. The ingredients of food have an immediate impact on human health, while production methods and supply chain management additionally contribute to the health of the environment. Novel food products seek legislation to make the food system more sustainable and healthy. The Food System Sciences Master’s degree course at the University of Bayreuth offers an interdisciplinary programme integrating different aspects of the food system, allowing students to gain insights into the interfaces between Food Chemistry, Human Physiology, Food Law, and the Bioeconomy. This interdisciplinary basis followed by in-depth specializations in one of the food system areas will allow you to make a significant contribution to ensure a sufficient, high quality food supply for the growing world population.

## Profile of the degree programme

**From interdisciplinarity to in-depth specializations for future food security**

The effects of climate change on the production of food become more evident year by year. In order to ensure a sufficient, high quality food supply in the future, different scientific disciplines need to work in close cooperation to allow a fast transformation of food systems for more sustainability and health. The unique interdisciplinary study program Food System Sciences at the University of Bayreuth trains students to bridge between disciplines. Building on this interdisciplinary basis, the program offers dedicated specializations in Analytics in Life Sciences, Nutritional Physiology, Food Law, and Bioeconomy, training highly qualified specialists with sought-after interdisciplinary skills.

**The study programme offers four specializations:**

1. The Analytics in Life Sciences specialization aims to provide a comprehensive and up-to-date overview of analytical research methods and applications, training students to work on analytical research questions including the basics of analytical quality assurance "From-Farm-to-Fork".

2. In the Nutritional Physiology specialization, students learn about the influence of nutrition on the human body, including the influence of defined diets on the development of non-communicable diseases or the interaction of nutrition and physical activity on the maintenance of health.

3. In the Food Law specialization, students learn European and international food law. This includes substantive and institutional food law. Key areas are dealt with in depth, enabling students to understand and help shape food-related regulatory approaches.

4. In the Bioeconomy specialization, students analyse the various aspects of sustainability in food systems from a production, business, and sociological perspective. The focus of the specialization is on plant-based foods using a holistic perspective to analyse production, distribution, and consumption.

## Career prospects

This degree programme aims to qualify students for Research and Development in both academic and company-based environments, for executive positions in the food industry and health-management, as well as for executive and consulting positions at authorities, in the media, and in other areas of administration and business.

### Admission requirements

Admission requirements
:   The admission requirement is a bachelor's degree (or equivalent) in natural sciences or life sciences or social sciences or law or economics or nutrition and health sciences with a final German grade of "2.5" or better.

Above-average interdisciplinary interest, which should be briefly stated in your application letter.

Language proficiency
:   [ENGLISH](https://www.international-office.uni-bayreuth.de/en/come-to-bayreuth/degree-students/evidence-of-language-proficiency/index.html): Advanced English language skills (at least B2 level, based on the Common European Framework of Reference of Languages)

[GERMAN](https://www.international-office.uni-bayreuth.de/en/come-to-bayreuth/degree-students/evidence-of-language-proficiency/index.html): German language skills (A1, can be obtained during the first year of the programme)

Admission procedure
:   Your qualification will be evaluated in an aptitude assessment process. The decision on the admission of the students is made by a committee on the basis of the applications received (two-stage aptitude test).
