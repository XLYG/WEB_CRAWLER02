## Profile of the degree programme

**A modern degree for meeting modern challenges**The programme views economics and philosophy and their integration as methods for analysing allocation, distributive, and institutional mechanisms in a world that is in need of new global governance structures. Chief among these are pressing foundational and practical questions: What are the dimensions of equality and justice? What are the economic and moral challenges that regional trade agreements create for the WTO? What is a fair allocation of voting power? Who bears responsibility for climate change? How do we encourage corporate social responsibility?

**Profile of the Master’s programme**This two-year international programme is jointly organized and taught by the Departments of Philosophy and Economics. It is the advanced degree for the prospective researcher, the national and international public policy analyst, and the concerned business professional. After
having gained a solid foundation in micro-, macro-, and empirical economics as
well as ethics, political philosophy, and philosophy of the social sciences,
the programme allows you to specialize according to your individual
interests and aptitude.

## Career prospects

**Fields of Employment**

Graduates of the MA in Philosophy & Economics pursue research careers in higher education or in the private and public sectors as well taking up public and private sector leadership and consulting positions.

For those interested in pursuing a doctorate at one of the University's faculties, the University of Bayreuth Graduate school provides supervision, teaching and training opportunities.

## Doctoral Research

### Doing a doctorate at the University of Bayreuth

The [University of Bayreuth Graduate School](https://www.win-ubt.uni-bayreuth.de/en/UBTGS-_-WiN/index.html) supervises doctoral students of all subjects who are pursuing a doctorate in a structured programme or at one of the University's seven faculties. We facilitate scientific exchange already in the earliest phase of independent research, and complement the interdisciplinary education of doctoral students with additional teaching and training opportunities. Our aim is to create the perfect research and learning conditions for them.

You can find all the information on doctoral studies in Bayreuth on the [homepage of the Junior Scholar Office WiN](https://www.win-ubt.uni-bayreuth.de/en/index.html).

### Admission requirements

Qualification
:   A degree (or completed course of studies) with an examination grade of “2.3” or better in the bachelor’s programmes Economics, Philosophy & Economics, or International Economics & Development at the University of Bayreuth, or an equivalent degree

Equivalency
:   If your degree is equivalent is decided by the admissions board. We welcome applications from all subjects with an economics component. If you have any questions regarding admission, please contact [pe-master@uni-bayreuth.de](mailto:pe-master@uni-bayreuth.de)

Language proficiency
:   [ENGLISH](https://www.international-office.uni-bayreuth.de/en/come-to-bayreuth/degree-students/evidence-of-language-proficiency/index.html): level B2
[GERMAN](https://www.international-office.uni-bayreuth.de/en/come-to-bayreuth/degree-students/evidence-of-language-proficiency/index.html): level A1 (to be completed by non-German speakers after the first year of study)

Applying without a degree certificate
:   Application may already be submitted before completion of the bachelor's degree; however, at total of at least 150 credits and a minimum grade of "good" (2.3) are required by the time of application.

Special conditions
:   Conditional admission is not offered for this programme
