## Online-Application

The application for a study program at TU Darmstadt has to be done online via the application portal TUCaN.
Please also note the information below on the DoSV procedure.

[Go to TUCaN (opens in new tab)](https://www.tucan.tu-darmstadt.de)

If you apply for the 1st semester of a degree programme that participates in the “[Dialogue-Oriented Service Procedure (DoSV)](https://dosv.hochschulstart.de/bewerber/6699;jsessionid=73A0BF6B1D568FC6EAC8377418E006749CEA4C7AEB3F75C9F44D6DF31ECD667A?DtBzP98ji7wEVgK3FCUDCu-aQ12vCa835NELSDAODDDpcTdoPTLsN0w7fTzAl6TAifCG6l6kkW8hHWQsAbULFMTLmilWiBD08CJhMFVz-aeuu647jg.=0)”, you must also register at [hochschulstart.de](http://www.hochschulstart.de/) beforehand. The study programmes that participate in the DoSV are listed [here.](https://www.tu-darmstadt.de/studieren/studieninteressierte/internationale_studieninteressierte/bewerbung_und_zulassung_international/index.en.jsp) This applies to German and European HZB.
