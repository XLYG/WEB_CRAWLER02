# Electrical Engineering and Information Technology (Q-Master ET/IT, teacher training), M.Ed.

## Program overview
The lateral entry master’s program in Electrical Engineering and Information Technology (Q-Master ET/IT) prepares you to teach electrical engineering and information technology at vocational schools. It allows individuals who have studied electrical engineering or a similar subject at a university or university of applied sciences to pursue a Masters of Education without having to complete a bachelor’s degree with teacher training first. This lateral entry master’s program focuses on education and teaching methodology in order to prepare you for later work as a teacher at a vocational school.Please note that this degree course is intended for individuals who wish to pursue a teaching degree in the future. It is not suitable if you are searching for a strictly technical degree course.Click here to go to the study program website:https://www.tu.berlin/go34913/
