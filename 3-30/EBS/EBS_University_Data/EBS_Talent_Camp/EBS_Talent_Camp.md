# EBS Talent Camp

Our promise

## Three days that will help you get ahead

Gain initial experience with degree programmes in the fields of economics and law at the Summer School and find out which course of study or career path suits you best. In addition to specialist insights, you will acquire specific key skills, from presentation techniques to confident public speaking.

At our three-day EBS Talent Camp, you will work on exciting case studies on the key topic of artificial intelligence in the fields of business and law. In addition, we combine academic knowledge with practical training, enabling you to develop key skills that are crucial for your studies and professional life. With additional team activities, accompanied by expert study advisors, this summer school is the best opportunity to enhance your personal development and support your career planning during the summer.

---

Concept in detail

During our three-day EBS Talent Camp, you will work on practical projects focusing on the key topics of artificial intelligence and sustainability, and develop your own solutions to real business challenges in interactive group work. In doing so, you will sharpen your analytical thinking, expand your presentation skills and deepen your fundamental knowledge of management and law. In practical data and presentation workshops, you will acquire the ability to make data-based economic decisions and to prepare complex content in a structured and convincing manner.

In addition, during the short programme you will improve your English skills and gain an authentic insight into student life at EBS, get to know our campus and meet with professors and students.

About EBS

## A university in the heart of Europe

Founded in 1971, EBS Universität is the oldest private business universitiy in Germany and is known for its competence, excellence, alumni, and business networks.

Under the aegis of the private, state recognised EBS Universität für Wirtschaft und Recht, EBS offers a unique portfolio consisting of Bachelor’s, Law, Master’s, and Doctoral programmes as well as first-rate executive education for working professionals. EBS is renowned for excellent research results together with a strong focus on practical application. It has been awarded the prestigious AACSB accreditation, placing it among the 12 AACSB accredited business schools in Germany. EBS Universität is highly ranked in the Financial Times for its Master in Finance and Master in Management programmes, as well as its strong Executive Education offerings.

With a focus on academic excellence and practical relevance, EBS provides top-tier education for students and professionals alike.
