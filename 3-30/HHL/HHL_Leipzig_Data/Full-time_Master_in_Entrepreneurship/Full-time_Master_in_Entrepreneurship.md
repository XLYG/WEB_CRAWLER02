### Five Reasons for the Master in Entrepreneurship at HHL

* **Do you have an idea but do not know how to found your start-up?** With us, you will learn how to turn your vision into a scalable business.
* **Are you missing the right environment?** You will find your start-up network here – from mentors to co-founders.
* **Do you want to be an entrepreneur, but still lack a brilliant idea?** With design thinking, trend radar and ideation sprints, you can find ideas that really have potential.
* **Are you afraid of taking a step into the unknown?** We accompany you from the idea to market entry – step by step.
* **What if your start-up fails?** Then you will take your experience, network and a top mentor with you into your next opportunity.

## Tuition fees

The tuition fees for the entire program amount to **EUR 38,500**. We offer a tuition fee discount if you submit your complete application by the following Early Bird deadlines\*:

* **Early Bird 1: November 30 – Discount: EUR 4,500**
* **Early Bird 2: January 31 – Discount: EUR 3,000**
* **Early Bird 3: March 31 – Discount: EUR 1,500**

This covers all tuition and examination costs and includes the tuition fees for the semester abroad at one of our partner universities. Of course, you do not have to pay the fees all at once. You pay your tuition fees in four instalments – spread over two years. For further information, see our payment terms.

**Note:** Your tuition fees are tax-deductible as anticipated income-related expenses. Check out our [**brochure**](https://www.hhl.de/app/uploads/2020/02/HHL_Flyer_Steuertipps.pdf) (in German) on this for tips. Please contact your tax consultant for more information.

\*In order to receive the early bird discount, you **will need to submit** your application by the above deadlines, as well as submit all missing documents and successfully pass the HHL admissions interview until January 31 (Early Bird 1), March 31 (Early Bird 2) or June 30 (Early Bird 3).

### Pay your tuition fees in installments!

After admission, you pay a deposit of EUR **3,000** . The remaining amounts are divided into four installments and are due on October 1 and March 1 of each academic year.
