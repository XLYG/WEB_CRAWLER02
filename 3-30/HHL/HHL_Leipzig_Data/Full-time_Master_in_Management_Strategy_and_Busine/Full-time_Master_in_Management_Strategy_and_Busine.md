## Master Strategy and Consulting in the Age of AI

Top consultants combine analytical thinking, strong communication, and the effective use of AI. The **HHL Master in Management | Strategy and Business Consulting Program** develops these capabilities across three key areas:

* **IQ:** Structured problem solving and data-driven decision-making
* **EQ:** Understanding client needs, clear communication, and team leadership
* **AQ:** Applying AI to generate insights and strategic recommendations

Through consulting projects, case discussions with industry experts, and hands-on learning, you will learn how to turn analysis into impactful strategies. The program is complemented by dedicated career counseling, international perspectives, and an inspiring study environment at
[HHL Campus](https://www.hhl.de/experience/life-at-hhl/campus/) in [Leipzig](https://www.hhl.de/experience/life-at-hhl/leipzig/).

## 4. Business Competencies

### Real-World Experience, Lasting Impact

Through consulting projects, case discussions with industry experts, and hands-on experiences, you’ll learn how to transform analysis into actionable strategies that create real impact for organizations. Whether it’s tackling real company challenges, studying abroad, or completing internships, the practical experience you gain, along with the connections and relationships you build, will serve you far beyond graduation.
Our program offers a range of unique opportunities to apply your newly acquired knowledge and skills, equipping you with the expertise and network to excel in strategy and consulting careers.

**Student Consulting Project**

This is a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to experience what it’s like to be a consultant. Your challenge is to collaborate in a team of four to six students to advise and support a company in solving a real-life problem. Working closely with both company representatives and HHL faculty, you will learn first-hand what it means to initiate real change.

[Explore the most recent projects in our blog](https://www.hhl.de/blog/student-consulting-projects-2021/)

**Internships**

Whether in strategy consulting, business development, or strategic management, our integrated internship gives you the chance to gain hands-on experience in diverse sectors and corporate cultures. It’s your opportunity to explore new perspectives, build valuable contacts, and develop skills aligned with your unique career goals. In addition to the mandatory internship, you can complete up to two additional internships to further deepen your practical experience.

## 5. Strategy and Business Consulting Deep Dives

### Become a consultant that stands out!

The Strategy and Business Consulting Focus is designed to turn you into a standout expert by combining academic excellence with practical insights into real-world business challenges. While diving deep into strategy, consulting frameworks, and hands-on projects, you will maintain a clear view of the big picture, enabling smart decision-making and impactful solutions for organizations.
The program develops the three core skills – Analytical Brilliance, Emotional Intelligence and AI Literacy – essential for tomorrow’s consultants, preparing you to excel in strategy and business consulting and make a real impact in your career.

## Tuition fees

The tuition fees for the complete program amount to **EUR 38,500**. We offer a tuition fee reduction if you submit your complete application by the following Early Bird deadlines\*:

* **Early Bird 1: November 30 – Discount: EUR 4,500**
* **Early Bird 2: January 31 – Discount: EUR 3,000**
* **Early Bird 3: March 31 – Discount: EUR 1,500**

This covers all tuition and examination costs and includes the tuition fees for the term abroad at one of our partner universities. And, of course, the fees do not have to be paid in one lump sum. You will pay your tuition fees in four instalments – spread out over two years. For further information, see our payment terms. One more important note: Your tuition fees are tax-deductible as an anticipated income-related expense. Check out our [brochure](https://www.hhl.de/app/uploads/2020/02/HHL_Flyer_Steuertipps.pdf) (in German) on this for tips. Please contact your tax consultant for more information.

\*In order to receive the early bird discount, you **will need to submit** your application by the above deadlines, as well as submit all missing documents and tests (including GMAT/GRE or HHL Entry Test) and successfully pass the HHL admissions interview until January 31 (Early Bird 1), March 31 (Early Bird 2) or June 30 (Early Bird 3).

## Payment policy

### **Pay your tuition fees in installments!**

After having been admitted to HHL, you are required to pay a down payment of EUR 3,000. The remaining fees will be paid as follows: The first and second installments are payable on October 1 and March 1 of the first study year. The third and fourth installments are payable on September 1 and March 1 of the second study year.
